# Analysis — Round 0 (work to date, reviewed from the PDF/PNG proofs)

Method: each piece re-read from its exported proof, not from memory of drawing it.
Flaws named here become constraints in the next round's prompts (see PROMPTS.md).

## What the body of work does well

- **Shading standard.** Radial-gradient hero shapes + translucent highlight/shadow
  passes (Fart Knocker portrait, bunny v2, all three Jolly pieces). This is the bar;
  anything flatter reads as a regression (bunny v1 proved it, got rebuilt).
- **Faces carry the pieces.** Monocle-and-glint on FK, heavy lids on the bunny,
  blush cores on Jolly — small specular marks are doing the emotional work.
- **Type is now an asset.** Virelai Sans lettering (crest, Jolly captions) has more
  character than the bundled faces; Mascots glyphs work as genuine ornaments
  (octopus on the crest, candles flanking the footer).

## Recurring flaws to design against

1. **Edge discipline.** Three separate incidents: bunny stain leaking past its
   badge rim, Jolly's wave arcs clipped by the canvas, crest burger silhouette
   reading as a capsule where it meets the medallion. → Prompt rule: state the
   safe margin and check the proof edges first.
2. **Badge compositions flatten shapes.** Inside a ring, curved forms get
   straightened by their neighbours (crest bun dome). → Give hero shapes air:
   shrink the subject ~10% inside any frame/badge.
3. **Symbol-marks that read as glyphs.** Jolly's fur tufts at the face edge read
   as little "ε/W" characters, not fur. → Texture marks must sit on texture,
   never straddle a boundary between two surfaces.
4. **Laurel leaves read as propellers** (crest) — paired leaves at one node cross
   into an X. → Stagger leaf pairs along the stem, vary sizes.
