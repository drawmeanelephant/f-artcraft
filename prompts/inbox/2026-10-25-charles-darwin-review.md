## Charles Darwin V1R00 — beards wrap the jaw; stroked side pieces read as flaps

- Kind: analysis
- Source: issue #1548; `work/scientists/draw_charles_darwin.py`,
  `art/scientists/charles-darwin/charles-darwin-V1R00.{vectorcraft,svg}`,
  proof `work/out/charles-darwin-V1R00@2x.png` (`check_rim.py`: clean)
- Applies to: scientist milestone cards; beards, temple hair, perch props
- Status: pending

### Evidence

Three loops.

- **A pointed beard is a wizard, a broad one is Darwin.** First pass
  ended the beard in a single point ~y650 — read as a goatee/mage
  beard. The elder's beard is a chest-covering mass: bottom edge
  flattened across ~55pt (x442–498 at y644–658), sides falling nearly
  straight from the cheeks.
- **Separately-stroked temple locks read as ear flaps.** Two
  ink-outlined white pieces beside the face at ear height read as
  earmuffs on both attempts. Fixed by dropping them entirely and
  raising the beard's top corners to the brim line, with two
  *unstroked* wedge fills bridging the gap — hair reads through the
  silhouette, not a second outline. Same lesson as the sleeve-bag:
  body-adjacent masses want to be one outline.
- **A shoulder finch is a cheap, clean prop:** plump tilted body oval,
  separate head, dark wing patch, conical beak, two 3pt stick legs, and
  a short perch-shadow stroke on the shoulder seam so the feet land
  somewhere. Facing the subject, ~85pt of it reads instantly at @2x.
- Milestone `ORIGIN OF SPECIES · 1859` in Source Sans 3, tracking 480
  → 490pt wide — longest line so far, still 70pt clear of the rim.

### Proposed change

For the milestone grammar: *beards and temple hair are part of the
head's outer mass (raise top corners, unstroked bridging fills), never
separate stroked pieces — a small bird on the shoulder needs only body
+ head + beak + wing patch + two leg strokes + a perch shadow.*
