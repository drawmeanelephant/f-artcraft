## Cheetah V1R00 — selling speed inside a static disc

- Kind: analysis
- Source: issue #1031; `work/animals/draw_cheetah.py`, `art/animals/cheetah/cheetah-V1R00.*`
- Applies to: animals field plates; motion subjects
- Status: pending

### Evidence

Proof `work/out/cheetah-V1R00@2x.png`. The rotary-gallop extension reads at
a glance: spine pulled flat, all four feet airborne, tail streamed level for
ballast. What sells the hundred is cheap: horizontal speed streaks at
opacity 14–26 behind the body, dust blobs kicked up under the hind feet,
and the low sun putting the horizon behind the cat. The tear mark — a 7pt
black stroke from eye corner past the muzzle — is the two-second species
tell; without it the head could be any small cat.

Iterated this cycle: a round ear perched tangent to the skull outline read
as a pom-pom; lowering it so ~a third of its base sits inside the head
silhouette makes it a laid-back ear (third confirmation of the
bigfoot/jaguar seating rule this session). Solid round spots, not rosettes
— the species split from #1030's broken rings held up.

What nearly failed: legs all dangling downward reads "flying squirrel"
before it reads "gallop" — the far/near pairs need one reaching and one
tucked to carry a real gallop frame. Belly tuck shading (`#8A5A1E` at
opacity 55) keeps the abdomen light without a second outline.

### Proposed change

For `prompts/series/20-field-plates.md`: motion subjects want the speed
read carried by environment props (dust at the trailing edge, streaks
behind, horizon low) rather than distorting the subject — the silhouette
stays field-guide clean while the plate does the running. Confirm the
ear-seating note from the jaguar note — it now has three cases.
