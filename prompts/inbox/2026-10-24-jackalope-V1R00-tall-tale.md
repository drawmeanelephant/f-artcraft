## Jackalope V1R00 — the small tell wants big treatment

- Kind: lesson
- Source: issue #1058; `work/animals/draw_jackalope.py`, `art/animals/jackalope/jackalope-V1R00.*`
- Applies to: animals field plates; hybrid/mythic subjects; thin geometry
- Status: pending

### Evidence

Proof `work/out/jackalope-V1R00@2x.png`. First pass gave the rabbit a
modest two-tine antler and on the proof it nearly vanished — an
incriminating speck, not a tell. The fix that survived: antlers drawn
*overbuilt* — two-pass strokes (INK edge at 8pt, bone at 5.5pt), a longer
beam, three tines, far antler offset behind. At plate scale a cryptid's
tell must be drawn larger than anatomy allows; nobody reads 4pt keratin.

The mirage mood rides on props: a faint upside-down twin (whole silhouette
at op 12, ears and antler points hanging down) floating above the left
horizon with two shimmer strokes slicing it, plus a cool "false water"
band laid right on the floor crest — the desert's promise of a lake. Cheap
and legible: one blob + ear spikes is enough for an upside-down ghost.

### Proposed change

For `prompts/series/20-field-plates.md`: for mythical/hybrid plates, the
impossible feature is the plate — overdraw it (thicker, longer, higher
contrast) rather than sizing it to the real anatomy. And mirage/fata
morgana effects want the inversion: a flipped ghost + a cool band on the
crest reads instantly; shimmer lines alone read as weather.
