- Source: issue #1121, `work/insects/blue-morpho-V1R00.py`,
  `art/insects/blue-morpho/blue-morpho-V1R00.{vectorcraft,svg,@2x.png}`
- Applies to: insects field plates (perched/feeding poses)
- Status: pending

### Evidence

First proof had the butterfly floating above the fallen fruit — legs ended
40pt short of the surface and the pose read as flight. Extending the legs
to touch the fruit top (plus an unrolled proboscis reaching it) is what
made the brief's "rests over a small fallen fruit" land. The blue field is
a radial gradient (light centre → deep rim) over a black border band with
cream margin spots; veins at 55% keep colour dominant per spec. The
underside reads through a single brown sliver of the far wing with a
three-eyespot row — enough to say the underside exists without splitting
the read of the blue.

### Proposed change

For perched insects, draw the support first and measure leg reach against
it in the proof — "rests on / feeding on" fails silently when the feet
hang in air. Candidate note for the insects section of
`prompts/series/20-field-plates.md`.
