## Camelback sofa V1R00 — show-wood legs die under deep rails

- Kind: lesson
- Source: #1158, `work/furniture/draw_camelback_sofa.py`, `art/furniture/camelback-sofa/`
- Applies to: field plates — furniture with exposed wood and tall legs
- Status: pending

### Evidence

First proof put a 50pt-deep upholstered-looking rail over leg tops at
y≈664; the cabriole S-curves collapsed into 30pt stubs that read as knobs.
Period Chippendale show-wood rails are slim: thinning the rail to ~40pt
and starting the knee higher (y≈648, still tucked under the rail) gave
the cabriole its swell-and-sweep read and room for a ball-and-claw foot
at plate scale. Damask sprigs stayed legible at opacity ~25; denser or
darker would have gone muddy.

### Proposed change

Furniture note candidate: when a piece has show-wood rails over shaped
legs, budget the rail to the slim period proportion and let the leg
silhouette own the base — a deep upholstered-looking rail swallows the
legs that identify the style. Pending until consolidated.
