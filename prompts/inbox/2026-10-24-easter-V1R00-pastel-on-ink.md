## easter-V1R00 — spring pastels read best on a deep warm-ink field

- Kind: lesson
- Source: issue #976, work/holidays/draw_easter.py, art/holidays/easter/easter-V1R00.*
- Applies to: holidays / festival seals
- Status: pending

### Evidence

Dyed-egg subjects in rose/sky/mint/lilac/butter pastels hold their colour
against a deep plum-ink disc (#3A2A34 -> #140E12); a pale field would wash
them out. The basket reads via contrast: dark weft rows over a light-to-dark
wicker gradient, plus a rolled rim drawn as two stacked strokes (ink 22 +
reed 16) that also tucks the eggs' bottoms into the cavity. Egg decorations
must respect the silhouette — band endpoints need an egg half-width estimate
(`rx*sqrt(1-(dy/ry)^2)*0.94`) or strokes leak past the outline.

### Proposed change

Worth recording in the Festival Seal grammar: pastel subjects want the dark
end of the palette range; "over the warm-ink field" means keep the disc
deep, not pastel.
