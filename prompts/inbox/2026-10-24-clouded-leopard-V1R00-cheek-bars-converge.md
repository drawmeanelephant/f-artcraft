## Clouded leopard V1R00 — cheek bars must stay on the cheek

- Kind: lesson
- Source: issue #1094; `work/animals/draw_clouded_leopard.py`,
  `art/animals/clouded-leopard/clouded-leopard-V1R00.*`
- Applies to: animals field plates; striped/barred cat faces
- Status: pending

### Evidence

Proof `work/out/clouded-leopard-V1R00@2x.png`. The signature double cheek
bars first ran straight down from the outer eye corners past the jaw — the
head contour curves inward there, so the bars exited the silhouette and
dangled beside the chin like sideburns, with small spots floating in the
gap between head and shoulder. Re-drawn as slim closed slivers that track
the inward curve of the cheek and converge at the jaw (~y540), they now
read as the real marking. A marking that should live inside a silhouette
must be routed along the silhouette, not plumb-lined past it.

The canopy-hold conceit worked: paws cresting the bough's mossy top edge
with claw ticks on the crest read as a grip, and the ringed tail flipped
over the bough's right end carried the species' famous tail without a
full-body pose. Tail spine points were kept to a ~400 radius envelope so
the hanging tip plus its stroke stays inside the rim.

### Proposed change

For `prompts/series/20-field-plates.md` known-traps: face markings drawn
as free strokes must be re-checked against the head silhouette at the
marking's own y — a bar that starts inside the face can still exit at the
jaw. Route it along the cheek's inward curve or end it at the edge; do not
let it dangle beside the chin.
