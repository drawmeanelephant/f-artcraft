- Applies to: milestone cards, horse breeds, gaited quadrupeds
- Status: pending

### Evidence

Shipped after two render-and-critique loops, built directly on the
arabian construction (one silhouette muzzle→dock, legs drawn under it).
The extended canter reads: near fore leads low and forward, far fore
trails folded, hinds stagger with the near leg driving off behind, and
the tail streams level with the spine. The bay's dark points were first
drawn as wide ellipses centred on each cannon — at plate scale they
swallowed the leg edges and rendered as club-foot blobs. The fix:
replace ellipses with narrow strokes running *down the inside of* each
cannon plus a slightly taller hoof/pastern cap — the point reads as dark
tapering instead of a lump.

Long flat muscle planes (thigh, forearm, barrel) are translucent fills
hugging the silhouette edges; the thin-coat sheen is a bright highlight
wedged along the topline. No tack, per the brief.

Survives critique as V1R00 with residue: the canter is a touch stiff —
the leading fore could carry one more fold at the knee for elastic feel;
the muzzle tip is slightly squared (a racer's muzzle tapers more); the
neck–withers junction still prints a shallow kink under the mane end.

### Proposed change

For the horse-breeds/milestone note at consolidation: *bay points and
any leg-level markings go inside the leg silhouette as narrow
longitudinal fills/strokes — an ellipse centred on a cannon overflows
both edges at plate scale and reads as a growth, not a marking.*
