## Pendant lamp V1R00 — enamel is a highlight, not a colour

- Kind: lesson
- Source: issue #1204, `work/furniture/draw_pendant_lamp.py`, `art/furniture/pendant-lamp/pendant-lamp-V1R00.*`
- Applies to: field plates — furniture
- Status: pending

### Evidence

The warehouse dome needed to read as *enamel*, not green paint: what did
it was two specular strokes on the left shoulder (a soft 6pt sweep + a
tight 2.4pt white line inside it) over the dark-to-mid horizontal ramp —
gloss is a hard-edged reflection, a gradient alone stays matte. The dome
is one lathe silhouette + a separate rolled-rim ellipse; drawing the rim
as its own element keeps the mouth bright underside honest. A pendant
plate needs somewhere for the light to land: a bare floor pool is weak —
the workbench top gives the pool a surface and the props (jar, rag,
tool) earn the "workshop mood" line. Cloth props at this scale drift
toward "bread loaf"; cool grey-blue + one fold line is the minimum read.
Cord: an S-curve smooth() at 5pt with an offset 1.6pt sheen line is
enough — a perfectly straight cord reads like a wire rod.

### Proposed change

Glossy materials (enamel, lacquer, glazed ceramic): reserve one hard
specular stroke pair per surface — gradient ramps alone read as matte.
Destination: `prompts/series/20-field-plates.md` furniture traps.
