## jardin-taburete-V1R00 — gaps read as sky unless shaded

- Kind: lesson
- Source: issue #1111, driver `work/furniture/jardin-taburete-V1R00.py`,
  native + proof `art/furniture/jardin-taburete/`
- Applies to: furniture field plates; any layered 3/4 construction study
- Status: pending

### Evidence

A near-side three-quarter chair in 2D flat coords. What worked: rear posts
run foot-to-backrest-top in single tapered quads (the seat covers the
joint); X-stitch lacing on the back band posts reads as rawhide immediately;
a translucent dark quad under the seat plane turns the seat-to-rail slits
into shade instead of seeing the disc through the chair.

What wobbles: receding feet end higher on the floor but a single shared
shadow ellipse leaves them floating — each far foot wants its own small
contact shade. The hide band's bottom edge must sag *down*, not bow up —
first pass smiled. Front-left leg to seat-corner registration needed a
second look; tapered `beam` quads hide the joint under the rail.

### Proposed change

For `prompts/core/` consideration: in 3/4 furniture views, every visible
joint should end either under another member or in shade — unshaded
construction gaps read as holes to the backdrop. Budget one under-plane
shadow shape per slab (seat, lid, shelf).
