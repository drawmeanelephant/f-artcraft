### Evidence

The first rack read as thin bat-wing crescents — megaloceros palms are
broad *upright fans*, not swept blades. Fixed by making each palm a tall
fan (base at the beam end, four tines ringing the outer-upper edge) with
soft groove strokes inside. Helper worth reusing: `antler(side)` mirrors a
canonical coordinate list through `X(x) = PCX + s*(x-PCX)` — one authored
side, both drawn. Brow tines: two per side read as rabbit ears; one small
shovel spike per side is the honest version. Bog water + cotton-grass puffs
(radial glow ellipses on thin stalks) gave the peat mood cheaply.

For `prompts/series/20-field-plates.md`: symmetric headgear/props should be
generated by a mirror function over canonical points — halves the authoring
and guarantees the spread stays balanced.
