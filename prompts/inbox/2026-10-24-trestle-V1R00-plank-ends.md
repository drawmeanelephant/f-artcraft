## Trestle table V1R00 — perspective plank ends read as knife points

- Kind: lesson
- Source: #1159, `work/furniture/draw_trestle_table.py`, `art/furniture/trestle-table/`
- Applies to: field plates — furniture, any slab seen in perspective
- Status: pending

### Evidence

A tabletop drawn as a trapezoid (long front edge, shorter back edge)
produces acutely pointed ends — the first proof read as a torpedo, not a
plank. Widening the deep front band past the surface ends helped the
lower silhouette but the surface plane still knifed out. The fix was
rounding the surface's own ends (quadratic caps between the back corner
and the front corner), so every layer of the slab — surface, breadboard
strips, edge band — has a blunt end. Tusk tenons and wedge pegs at the
post faces read as joinery at plate scale.

### Proposed change

Furniture note candidate: any slab in slight perspective needs rounded or
overhanging end caps on every layer — a bare trapezoid's acute corners
read as points at plate scale. Pending until consolidated.
