## Trundle bed V1R00 — sell the pull-out with a drawer face

- Kind: lesson
- Source: issue #1202, `work/furniture/draw_trundle_bed.py`, `art/furniture/trundle-bed/trundle-bed-V1R00.*`
- Applies to: field plates — furniture
- Status: pending

### Evidence

The trundle is a second bed that has to read as *stored*, not just lower.
What carried it: the proud drawer face (fielded panels + brass knobs +
casters with hub dots) in front of the frame's near rail, with the thin
pad mattress ending just shy of the rail line so it visibly slides under.
The dark slot wash under the rail (a flat quad at the shadow layer) is
what the trundle is pulled *out of* — without it the piece reads as two
beds, not a bed and its drawer. A duvet flap draped over the near rail
needed a hem line + one crease to stop reading as a painted panel;
unbroken prism faces at fabric scale always want one stroke to become
cloth. Ordering note: anything that hangs in front of a rail (drape,
cloth) draws after the rail even when it belongs to the bedding group.

### Proposed change

For nested/stow-away furniture (trundle, nesting tables done, sofa bed):
draw the stored unit proud of the frame with drawer furniture (face,
pulls, casters) and keep one dark slot of the gap it came from.
Destination: `prompts/series/20-field-plates.md` furniture traps.
