## american-bison V1R00 — species read lives in silhouette depth, not detail

- Kind: lesson
- Source: #1037, work/animals/draw_american_bison.py, art/animals/american-bison/american-bison-V1R00.*
- Applies to: animals field plates; large mammals with a diagnostic outline
- Status: pending

### Evidence

First pass kept the head high and level with the back: the plate read as a
generic hoofed loaf. Dropping the muzzle to near the drift, steepening the
face front, and cresting the hump behind the poll fixed the read at thumbnail
size. A hard zigzag "cape fringe" marking where the shag ends drew a visible
seam; at opacity ~38 with loose guard-hair strokes over it, it became pelage.

### Proposed change

For `prompts/series/20-field-plates.md` known traps: get the species'
silhouette first (head height vs shoulder crest); decorative boundary marks
between coat regions should stay under ~40 opacity or they draw as seams.
