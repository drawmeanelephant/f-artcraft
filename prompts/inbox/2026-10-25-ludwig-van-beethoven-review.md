## ludwig-van-beethoven V1R00 — mane locks must grow out of the silhouette

- Kind: lesson / analysis
- Source: issue #1529; `work/musicians/draw_ludwig_van_beethoven.py`,

- Applies to: milestone cards / any "hair is the likeness" portrait
- Status: pending

### Evidence

Three render-and-critique loops on the mane:

1. A lobed cloud drawn *above* the head with a separate inner hairline
   read as a floating cap; leaf-shaped "locks" stuck on the edge read as
   ears/antennae. Any hair tuft drawn as a separate closed blob with a
   blunt rounded tip reads as a body part, not hair.
2. Building the turbulence into the mane path itself — alternating
   out/in point pairs along the crown — gives a wild silhouette that
   still reads as one mass. That worked.
3. Long pointed "flame" strands off the crown still read as horns at
   plate scale. Removed; the zigzag edge carries the wildness alone.

What survived: mane wraps the skull and frames both cheeks to the jaw;
hairline sits just above the brows so the frown (thick low brows, two
furrow strokes, downturned mouth + corner ticks) stays readable. The
collar is one band drawn over the cravat sides, which merges the two
shapes — separate wing shapes read as a bow tie. Score sheet on the
floor chord at 62% opacity gives the world cue without noise.

`check_rim` clean; milestone line `THE NINTH · 1824` 296pt wide, clears
the lower arc by ~65pt.

### Proposed change

For hair-driven likenesses: build the wild silhouette into the mass
outline (alternating out/in pairs), never as separate protruding
shapes. Separate strands off the silhouette must be long relative to
their width and clearly swept — short ones read as horns. Destination:
the milestone-cards note in `prompts/series/` at next consolidation.
