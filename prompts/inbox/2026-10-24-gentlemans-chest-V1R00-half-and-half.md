## Gentlemans chest V1R00 — split the front face, not the silhouette

- Kind: lesson
- Source: issue #1196, `work/furniture/draw_gentlemans_chest.py`,
  `art/furniture/gentlemans-chest/gentlemans-chest-V1R00.{vectorcraft,svg,@2x.png}`
- Applies to: field plates — furniture (hybrid / two-part case pieces)
- Status: pending

### Evidence

The "half wardrobe, half chest of drawers" brief read best as one calm
silhouette with a split front: fielded wardrobe door left (hinge tabs on
the jamb side, ring pull + escutcheon on the latch side) against a column
of four small drawers right, then two deep drawers full-width below — the
compromise is told inside a single case outline. Campaign codes that held
at plate scale: flush ring pulls (brass square bezel + dark recess + a
hanging ring arc, three shapes), brass L-straps at the eight case corners,
and a leather carry loop on the side face as a shallow U between two brass
mounts. Field props (binoculars = two short barrels + bridge + focus dot,
folded map quad with fold strokes) pin the scale. Caption dodges the
Virelai apostrophe-ring trap by writing GENTLEMANS.

### Proposed change

Furniture spec candidates: "Hybrid pieces read as one silhouette with a
divided front — don't stack two cases." and "Campaign hardware kit: flush
ring pull (bezel, recess, hanging ring) + corner L-straps + a leather side
loop carry the campaign code without uniforms or badges." Pending.
