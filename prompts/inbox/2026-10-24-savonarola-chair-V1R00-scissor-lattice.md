## Savonarola chair V1R00 — the X-lattice only reads near side-on

- Kind: lesson
- Source: #1213, `work/furniture/draw_savonarola_chair.py`, `art/furniture/savonarola-chair/savonarola-chair-V1R00.vectorcraft`, proof `work/out/savonarola-chair-V1R00@2x.png`
- Applies to: furniture series — lattice / trellis / spindle sides
- Status: pending

### Evidence

Three failed takes before the side lattice read as a folding scissor. At the
house rig's usual TH=30° the side face is so foreshortened that any
depth-diagonal collapses to a near-vertical line — the weave rendered as
hanging fringe twice over (first a sine anti-phase weave, then a
pivot-pair scissor). TH=60–70° puts the lattice plane near face-on and the
X cells finally show. What also mattered: slats must span *two bays and
cross neighbours at quarter points* (the folding-gate chain) — pairs that
only cross their own mirror read as clutter; splayed feet needed a floor
runner to stop looking like frayed rope. A warm spine edge-light
(~2.6pt, opacity ~45) keeps thin slats separate at plate scale.

### Proposed change

For `prompts/series/` furniture notes: when a piece's signature is a side
lattice (scissor chairs, trestle gates, cot sides), rotate the rig to
TH≈60–70° and let the front/back faces go nearly edge-on; structure the
lattice as a folding-gate chain (bay row, slats spanning 2 bays, pins at
quarter-point crossings), and ground the feet with a runner rail rather
than bare splayed ends.
