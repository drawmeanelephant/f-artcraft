## Morgan V1R00 — high carriage comes from the poll, not the head

- Kind: analysis
- Source: issue #1523; `work/horse-breeds/draw_morgan.py`,
  `art/horse-breeds/morgan/morgan-V1R00.{vectorcraft,svg}`,
  proof `work/out/morgan-V1R00@2x.png`
- Applies to: horse-breeds milestone cards; standing show-stance subjects
- Status: pending

### Evidence

One render-and-critique pass; shipped from the now-proven recipe.

What worked:

- "High head carriage" is the crest angle: poll up near y≈380 with the
  face near-vertical gives the proud Morgan look; the head itself
  doesn't need to tip. Pair with hind legs parked a half-step back
  (hooves trailing behind the hip line) and the stance reads as a
  calm show pose, not mid-stride.
- Tail carried high: dock the tail ABOVE the croup line and let it
  fall in a flag — cheap, unmistakable.
- Black points on bay: dark cannons + mane/tail is the whole coat
  story; no interior markings needed.
- A short two-rail fence on the left floor stretch is the Vermont
  pasture cue at ~6 calls and 45% opacity; keep decor BEHIND the
  subject's legs so it can't tangle the silhouette.
- Rolling-hill silhouette on the skyline (same device as the mustang
  mesa) gives the horizon without a horizon line.

Watch for:

- A mane drawn thick over the poll can swallow the head/neck
  boundary; keep its leading edge behind the ear line or the face
  reads hooded.

### Proposed change

Card grammar stands; add to the horse-breeds note at consolidation:
show stance = high poll + vertical face + parked hinds; decor sits
behind leg level. Series now has one card per stance family
(gallop / trot / square-stand / parked-stand).
