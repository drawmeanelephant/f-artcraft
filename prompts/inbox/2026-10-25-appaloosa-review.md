## Appaloosa V1R00 — the blanket is a sheet, the spots live inside it

- Kind: analysis
- Source: issue #1518; `work/horse-breeds/draw_appaloosa.py`,
  `art/horse-breeds/appaloosa/appaloosa-V1R00.{vectorcraft,svg}`,
  proof `work/out/appaloosa-V1R00@2x.png`
- Applies to: milestone cards, horse breeds, patterned/marked quadrupeds
- Status: pending

### Evidence

Shipped on the first render after standing up the shared
single-silhouette recipe. The dalmatian's rule carried straight over:
each spot is one hand-placed irregular blob (`spot()` helper copied
nearly verbatim), never a texture fill. The blanket is a separate white
sheet fill draped over loin and rump with a ragged edge; spots sit
inside it and three strays creep forward onto the flank — that creep is
what keeps it reading as coat, not a saddle pad. Mottled muzzle is a
soft pink-grey patch plus four tiny freckles around the nostril; striped
hooves are two 3pt pale strokes on each dark hoof cap; the eye gets the
breed's visible white sclera as a pale ellipse behind the dark almond.

The Palouse cue is two faint rolling-hill contour strokes high on the
disc at 25–30% opacity — horizon lines that stay well above the
subject's head and clear the rim check.

Survives critique as V1R00 with residue: the sclera ring reads slightly
large at @2x — could come down a few points; the blanket's lower edge is
a bit tidy (a couple of scallops or a drip would feel more grown-in);
the tail could sit lower on the dock.

### Proposed change

For the horse-breeds/milestone note at consolidation: *appaloosa blanket
= white sheet fill over the silhouette + hand-placed spot blobs inside
it + 2–3 strays escaping the blanket edge; the strays are what sells
it.* Also: *visible sclera is a pale ellipse UNDER the almond eye, not
around it.*
