## Umbrella stand V1R00 — furled umbrellas hang tip-down

- Kind: lesson
- Source: #1233, `work/furniture/draw_umbrella_stand.py`, `art/furniture/umbrella-stand/umbrella-stand-V1R00.vectorcraft`, proof `work/out/umbrella-stand-V1R00@2x.png`
- Applies to: furniture series — vessels with contents sticking out
- Status: pending

### Evidence

First pass drew the canopies wide at the mouth tapering toward the handle —
they read as tusks or hockey sticks, not umbrellas. A stored umbrella is
ferrule-down: thin tip vanishing into the mouth, canopy widening to its
shoulder just below the shaft, then shaft, then the J-crook. Flipping the
ribbon taper (2.4 at the foot to ~12 at the shoulder) fixed the read in one
pass. The dark mouth ellipse drawn *over* the canopy feet is what seats
them in the stand rather than resting on the rim.

### Proposed change

For `prompts/series/` furniture notes: for container pieces (umbrella
stand, vase, planter), draw contents dipping *into* a mouth ellipse and
re-assert the interior darkness over the content feet — and sanity-check
taper direction against the object stored, not the object in use.
