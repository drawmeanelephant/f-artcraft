## Fijian kali headrest V1R00 — ground the feet inside the shadow

- Applies to: furniture field plates
- Status: pending

### Evidence

Drawn from Te Papa OL002079 (wood, 480mm long, Oldman Collection): a single
long slightly waisted beam — the neck rest — on a splayed leg pair at each
end, rounded lugs, polished top sheen, no invented carving or timber claim.
Warm wood on an ocean-night disc with moon glow and sparse stars.

First pass floated the piece: the contact shadow sat ~25pt below the foot
tips so the beam read as hovering. Raising the shadow until its top edge
met the feet and extending the far-side legs down to the same implied
ground plane fixed the grounding; the far feet still land higher on canvas,
which sells the low three-quarter view.

### Proposed change

For furniture plates: the contact shadow's top edge must meet or overlap
the lowest foot tip — a shadow wholly below the feet makes the object levitate.
Candidate for the field-plates traps list.
