## Secretary desk V1R00 — front view carries a complex case piece

- Kind: lesson
- Source: issue #1161, `work/furniture/draw_secretary_desk.py`, `art/furniture/secretary-desk/secretary-desk-V1R00.*`
- Applies to: field plates — furniture
- Status: pending

### Evidence

Drawn dead-front rather than the usual three-quarter: a secretary is a
stack of stories (cornice → glazed bookcase → open fall cavity → drawer
bank → bracket feet), and symmetry reads cleaner than perspective at
plate scale. The fall front is the signature — drawn open as a shallow
trapezoid with a green leather inset, its hinge dots at the cavity lip;
the pigeonhole gallery behind it is what makes the piece a *secretary*
and not a bookcase. Books behind the glazing needed ~62% opaque spines
under ~20% glass to read through; mullions at 9pt hold at @2x.

### Proposed change

For multi-storey case pieces (secretaries, highboys, linen presses):
consider a front view — the elevation *is* the design. Spend the
three-quarter budget instead on one opened element (a fall front, a
drawer, a door ajar) so the interior craft shows.
