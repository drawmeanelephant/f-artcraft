## Akhal-Teke V1R00 — metallic is an edge treatment, not a material

- Kind: analysis

- Status: pending

### Evidence

Shipped on the first render. The metallic read is a stack of cheap
parts: a warm gold coat ramp (never flat), a narrow hard `SHEEN` wedge
wedged just inside the topline edge, a crest sheen stroke, and one
thin gleam on the shoulder and jaw — every element hugs the silhouette
edge, per the wapiti/friesian rule. Nothing mid-body, and nothing
needs to be: at plate scale "gilded" = sharp bright edges on gold.

Buckskin tells that carry the breed: dark mane/tail points, the dorsal
stripe stroked along the topline edge, and dark cannon fills — narrow
longitudinal shapes INSIDE the leg silhouette (the thoroughbred rule:
never an ellipse centred on the cannon). The sparse mane is a thin
crest band ~15pt deep, not the scalloped baroque fall.

Type traits: long thin neck, slim body, thin legs (~32pt tops vs the
draft's 44–48), abdomen tucked up toward the flank, small refined
head. Rose plate + low sun + dune contours = the Karakum world for
~10 calls.

Survives critique as V1R00 with residue: the far hind sits close
enough to the near to read as one leg at thumbnail size; the
forelock wisp lands a touch low over the brow; the dorsal stripe
gets partially swallowed by the gilded band under it — pulling them
a few points apart would help at @2x.

### Proposed change

For the horse-breeds card note at consolidation: *"metallic" at plate
scale = gold ramp + sharp sheen wedges pinned to the silhouette edges
+ dark points; do not chase interior gradients — they read as
stains.* No structural divergence.
