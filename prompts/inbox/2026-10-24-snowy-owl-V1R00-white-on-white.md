## Snowy owl V1R00 — white-on-white needs the ink outline and inside-out barring

- Kind: analysis
- Source: issue #1045; `work/animals/draw_snowy_owl.py`,
  `art/animals/snowy-owl/snowy-owl-V1R00.*`
- Applies to: animal field plates, pale-subject-on-pale-plate cases
- Status: pending

### Evidence

The brief ("a white owl out of white sky") pushes the palette to the pale
end of the plate grammar. What carried it: the radial plate stays a step
darker than the bird's white dome, the 6pt slate ink outline does the
separating work, and the gold irises are the only warm note. First render
had the crown-bar chevrons placed outside the head silhouette — they read
as stray marks floating on the sky. Moved inside the silhouette edge (and
checked on the proof) the same marks read as plumage. The male's sparse
barring plus the hooded brow gives the glare the species is known for.
Snowdrift closes on the `A420,420` arc; no rim leaks; name fits.

### Proposed change

On pale-subject plates, the ink outline is load-bearing, not decoration —
budget it ≥5pt. Any decorative mark intended as plumage must sit strictly
inside the silhouette: on light fields, marks outside the edge read as
sky noise, not texture.
