## Red-eyed tree frog V1R00 — front-on squat carries the species

- Kind: analysis
- Source: issue #1044; `work/animals/draw_red_eyed_tree_frog.py`,
  `art/animals/red-eyed-tree-frog/red-eyed-tree-frog-V1R00.*`
- Applies to: animal field plates
- Status: pending

### Evidence

Front-on squat was chosen over the usual side profile so both red eyes
face the viewer — the species' one non-negotiable signal. On the proof the
eyes (socket bulge + radial red iris + vertical slit pupil) carry the
identification alone; the blue flanks with three cream bars and the orange
toe pads confirm it. Rain-night mood holds: mist glow fades before the rim
via per-stop opacity, rain streaks at opacity ~15, wet beads on leaf and
back. Floor closes on the disc's own `A420,420` arc; no leaks, name
`RED-EYED TREE FROG` at 52pt measures 657pt wide inside the 800pt limit.

Weaknesses: the pose is near-symmetric and reads slightly static; the two
nostril dots plus the back rain bead form an accidental triangle of dots on
the upper head. The piece survives its critique — signals read, mood holds,
grammar intact.

### Proposed change

For amphibian/reptile plates where the face is the signal, prefer front-on
or near-front poses even when the body grammar favours profiles: the eyes
must be countable. Keep single light accents (beads, glints) off the face
centerline so they can't be misread as anatomy.
