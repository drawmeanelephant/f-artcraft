## Caballito de totora V1R00 — bundle ends need bluntness, not points

- Kind: analysis
- Source: issue #1115; driver `work/vehicles/caballito-de-totora-V1R00.py`;
  native and proofs `art/vehicles/caballito-de-totora/` +
  `work/out/caballito-de-totora-V1R00.*`
- Applies to: vehicles / watercraft field plates
- Status: pending

### Evidence

Huanchaco form per the PROMPERÚ brief: two lashed totora bundles, inner one
drawn up into the hooked prow, blunt bound stern, plain paddle with blade in
a small contained water patch (not a full chord segment — the brief asked
for a contained reflection, and the ellipse patch keeps the sea-green disc
open around the hull).

First pass put a sharp-pointed sliver for the second bundle that read as a
tiny sail or stray plank — exactly what the brief warns against. Rounding
its end into a blunt cap made it read as the smaller sibling bundle. A pair
of crossing "throat lashings" at the neck base rendered as a bow-tie; one
band perpendicular to the neck axis is the right call. Reed lines must run
into the prow tip or the hooked neck reads as a separate part glued on.

### Proposed change

For bundled/lashed subjects: end every sub-element in a blunt bound cap,
never a point — points read as hardware or sails. Bands wrap perpendicular
to the member's axis. Candidate for the field-plates traps list.
