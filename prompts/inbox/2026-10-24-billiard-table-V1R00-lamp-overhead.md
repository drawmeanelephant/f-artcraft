## Billiard table V1R00 — a hanging fixture fills the dead air over low furniture

- Kind: lesson
- Source: issue #1193, `work/furniture/draw_billiard_table.py`,
  `art/furniture/billiard-table/billiard-table-V1R00.{vectorcraft,svg,@2x.png}`
- Applies to: field plates — furniture (any low, wide subject)
- Status: pending

### Evidence

A billiard table at pitch ~27° leaves the whole upper half of the disc empty;
the fix that also carried the brief's "chalk-dust mood" was a three-shade
pendant lamp inside the disc: bar + conical shades at ~y200, suspension rod
faded to opacity 0 well clear of the rim, and a warm translucent cone
spreading onto the bed. Twelve low-opacity dots in the cone read as chalk
dust. Drop-pocket baskets drawn at apron height were fully occluded — they
must hang ~3in below the apron's bottom edge to peek into view at the near
corners. The eight-ball's "8" needed Source Sans 3 (Virelai Sans has no
digit glyphs) at ~0.6× ball radius.

### Proposed change

Furniture note candidate: "Low wide pieces (tables, chests, beds) may hang
one fixture in the empty upper disc — a pendant lamp, a canopy, a wall
frame — suspended by a support that fades out before the rim. Props that
would be hidden by an apron or rail must crest or hang visibly past it."
Pending until consolidated.
