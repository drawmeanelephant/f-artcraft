## Egg chair V1R00 — a late shade ellipse landed on the cushion

- Kind: lesson
- Source: issue #1184, `work/furniture/draw_egg_chair.py`, `art/furniture/egg-chair/egg-chair-V1R00.*`
- Applies to: field plates — furniture (upholstered forms)
- Status: pending

### Evidence

First proof put the cocoon read in place: domed shell + inner facing inset
up-left + cove shadow under the dome + wing lobes reads unmistakably as a
swivel pod on an aluminium star. But a "shell base shade" ellipse meant
for the shell–column gap was drawn last, after the seat cushion, and sat
on the cushion as a dark blob — a hole in the seat, not a joint shadow.
Cut rather than repositioned: the shell's own gradient already shades the
tuck-under, so the orphan shade had no surface to belong to.

### Proposed change

For `prompts/series/` furniture notes: a shadow accent must sit on the
surface it darkens and be drawn adjacent to it in z-order — a shade added
at the end "for depth" usually lands on the wrong form. If deleting it
loses nothing, it was never needed.
