### Evidence

A candle altar alone left a dark hole mid-field. A single marigold arch
(`marigold_at` along a quadratic from (264,620) over (500,356) to
(736,620)) frames the ofrenda and fills the void — the ofrenda's real-world
arco de flor. Legs should end just inside the altar's marigold bank so the
arch reads as planted.

Papel picado flags hang off a quadratic swag: rotate each flag ~0.4× the
cord's local slope so they sag with the string rather than hanging level.
Punched holes = tiny diamonds/dots filled with the field colour — cheaper
than real cut-outs and reads at plate scale.

The pan de muerto reads at ~66px wide; at 104px it swallows the votives.
Objects on a crowded altar need staggered heights: two tall outer candles
(108–120), two short inner (66–76), loaf centre-front.

### Proposed change

For plate grammar notes: "a still life wants an overhead frame — arch,
swag or hanging garland — drawn *before* the objects it frames".
