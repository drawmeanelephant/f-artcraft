## Common raven V1R00 — fingered primaries are scallops, not zigzags

- Kind: lesson
- Source: issue #1078; `work/animals/draw_common_raven.py`,
  `art/animals/common-raven/common-raven-V1R00.*`
- Applies to: field plates, birds in flight
- Status: pending

### Evidence

First pass built spread wingtips as sharp `L`-segment zigzags along the
trailing edge — on the proof they read as bat-wing barbs pointing the wrong
way, not feathers. The fix: convex `Q` lobes separated by concave in-cuts
(tip out, notch back, tip out), each lobe a rounded primary splayed around
the tip direction — instantly reads as the corvid/raptor "hand". Two other
reads that only survived as merged silhouettes: the heavy bill had to be one
stroked shape with the head (a separate lens-shaped bill floated like a
leaf), and the throat hackles wanted a small filled zigzag tuft, not long
strokes (long strokes read as dangling ribs). The dashed corkscrew trail
(`dash=[5,10]`) behind the bird tells "barrel roll" without a second bird.

### Proposed change

For flight plates: wingtip fingers = alternating convex-lobe/concave-notch
`Q` pairs splayed along the tip fan; head+bill as one silhouette whenever
the bill is heavy. Destination: animals section of the field-plate rules.
