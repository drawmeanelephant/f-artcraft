## German shepherd V1R00 — mask + nape + saddle is one continuous black

- Kind: analysis
- Source: issue #1501; `work/dog-breeds/draw_german_shepherd.py`,
  `art/dog-breeds/german-shepherd/german-shepherd-V1R00.{vectorcraft,svg}`,
  proof `work/out/german-shepherd-V1R00@2x.png` (`check_rim.py`: clean)
- Applies to: dog-breeds milestone cards; saddle/pattern markings
- Status: pending

### Evidence

Two and a half loops. Draft 1 repeated the labrador's mistake in
profile: a separate muzzle capsule beside a skull blob read as a
duckbill. The labrador lesson transferred directly — one head silhouette
from nose tip through brow and crown back under the jaw fixed it.

The breed code is the *continuous* black: the mask (muzzle top + eye
surround) runs into a black nape patch behind the skull, which opens
into the saddle blanket to the hips. Draft 1 drew only the blanket and
it read as a rug strapped on; adding the nape patch between head and
saddle is what turned "tan dog with a rug" into a shepherd. Ears are
simple tall triangles, the near one leaning slightly forward — erect set
read on the first pass.

Neck first draft pinched where it met the chest (fawn-like); widening
the front edge ~15pt fixed the stack. Hind legs got one hard bend at
the hock per the quadruped-leg rule.

### Proposed change

For the grammar note: *on patterned breeds, markings connect into a
single logical zone (mask → nape → saddle), not islands; a strap-edge
silhouette reads as clothing, a merged one reads as coat.*
