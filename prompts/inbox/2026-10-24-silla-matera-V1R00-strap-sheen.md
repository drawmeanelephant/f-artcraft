## Silla matera V1R00 — woven strap seats: one direction continuous, one segmented

- Kind: lesson / analysis
- Source: issue #1108, `work/furniture/silla-matera-V1R00.py`,
  `art/furniture/silla-matera/silla-matera-V1R00.*`
- Applies to: furniture field plates; any woven-seat piece
- Status: pending

### Evidence

The tiento weave is faked cheaply and it works: front-to-back strips drawn
as continuous sagging ribbons, left-to-right strips drawn only in the gaps
between them (segments tucked "under"). At plate scale the eye completes
the weave — no per-crossing alternation needed. Each strap is a ribbon
path with its own 3-stop rawhide gradient, alternating three pale tones
row to row so the strips don't merge into one sheet.

What failed and was cut: a straight "woodgrain sheen" line laid near the
near front leg. The leg splays in projection (3 joints), the sheen was a
2-joint segment, so it floated off the member and read as a dangling
rawhide offcut below the seat. Lesson: decorative sheens must share the
member's own polyline or be dropped; a highlight that misses its member
reads as a foreign object.

Joinery reads: short splayed pole legs, low side + front stretchers, two
backrest rails carried on extensions of the rear legs — the posts rise
through the seat, which is the rustic-tell the brief asked for.

### Proposed change

For `prompts/series/20-field-plates.md` furniture notes: "Woven seats:
draw one strap direction continuous over a dark under-bed, the crossing
direction segmented in the gaps — draw order does the weave. Keep
highlights on the member's own joints; a sheen that leaves the member
reads as a loose strap."
