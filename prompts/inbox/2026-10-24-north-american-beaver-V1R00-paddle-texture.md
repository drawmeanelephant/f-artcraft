# North american beaver V1R00 — the paddle tail needs the crosshatch to read

- Kind: lesson
- Source: issue #1072; `work/animals/draw_north_american_beaver.py`,

  textured signature parts
- Status: pending

### Evidence

Proof `work/out/north-american-beaver-V1R00@2x.png`. The tail splayed flat
on the mud behind the sitting body reads instantly — but only because the
crosshatch hatch lines inside it say SCALY. A smooth dark paddle is a
beaver tail for nobody; the texture IS the species code. The held-stick +
orange-incisor + pencil-point-stump triangle carries the behaviour read:
three small props around the mouth beat a more elaborate body pose. Also
noted: a pale wedge pair angled at the mouth corner (incisor) must be
drawn ON TOP of the stick stroke, or the stick hides the bite point.

### Proposed change

For `prompts/series/20-field-plates.md` known-traps: *signature parts
whose identity lives in texture (scaly tail, quills, scales) need the
texture drawn in — the silhouette alone is ambiguous. And behaviour
props cluster at the head: stick in the paws + incisor at the mouth +
gnawed stump nearby reads as a verb, not three nouns.*
