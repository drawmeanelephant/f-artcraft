### Evidence

Shipped on the first proof. What worked:

- **Off-balance on purpose**: figure centre moved to x≈455 and a tall
  apparatus mass parked at x≈675 leans the plate right — reads as
  intentional because the name line stays centred and the light pool
  stays left. Briefs that ask for asymmetry want a counterweight, not a
  tilt: shift the subject, anchor one heavy prop on the other side.
- **The apparatus reads through the window**: dome-topped cryostat
  column + base flange + cap ring + gauge dial + a chamber window with a
  radial glowing source. The parity gag is inside the chamber — three
  beta strokes spraying down-left, one faint one up. Keep emission
  decor inside the vessel; loose particles would read as rim leaks.
- **Detector ring = two arcs**: a fat front arc (w7, ~85%) and a dimmer
  back arc (w5, ~50%) above it wraps the column without an ellipse that
  would have to pass behind the chamber window.
- **Wardrobe layering**: coat silhouette first, qipao panel over the
  centre, then thin `Coat edge` strokes where the fronts part. The
  mandarin collar + diagonal frog closure is both period-correct and a
  second asymmetry rhyme with the physics.
- **Hand rests on a ledge**: a small flange ledge drawn on the column's
  edge gives the outstretched hand somewhere to land — floating
  near-touches read as missed grips.
- Long coat + dress can run to the floor band: when the hem covers the
  calves, skip drawing legs/shoes at all (here they drew under the hem
  harmlessly, but they're invisible).

### Proposed change

For the consolidated milestone note (destination `prompts/series/`):
"apparatus scene" recipe — subject shifted ~45pt off centre, one tall
instrument on the far side standing on the same floor band, its glow
colour becomes the card's rim-light source. Emission decor stays inside
the vessel.
