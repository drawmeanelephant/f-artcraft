## Fiddler crab V1R00 — the semaphore claw wants scale, the echo wants a ground line

- Kind: lesson
- Source: issue #1086; `work/animals/draw_fiddler_crab.py`, `art/animals/fiddler-crab/fiddler-crab-V1R00.*`
- Applies to: animals field plates; asymmetric crabs, background fauna
- Status: pending

### Evidence

Proof `work/out/fiddler-crab-V1R00@2x.png`. The major claw carries the
species code and survives being roughly a third of the carapace width:
bulbous manus + raised dactyl + drooping fixed finger, orange against a
blue-green shell, ink outline throughout. First pass failed twice and was
fixed before shipping: the carapace was a plain egg — squaring the top
corners and flattening the crown sold the fiddler's boxy shell; and the
distant answering male floated mid-plate reading as random mud clods —
moving it onto the floor line (~y660) with two 3pt eyestalk strokes turned
it back into a crab silhouette.

Feeding pellets (small stroked ellipses) and a low dark burrow ellipse sell
the mudflat better than extra ripples; the ripples went in anyway at op. 45
and don't fight them.

### Proposed change

For `prompts/series/20-field-plates.md`: background fauna must sit on the
floor/horizon line, never mid-field — an ungrounded silhouette reads as
debris. Give it the subject's own tell (here, the raised claw) at reduced
opacity and it reads as an echo, not a smudge.
