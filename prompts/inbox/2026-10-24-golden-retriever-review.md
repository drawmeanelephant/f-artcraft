## Golden retriever V1R00 — the bib must not bury the forelegs

- Kind: analysis
- Source: issue #1502; `work/dog-breeds/draw_golden_retriever.py`,
  `art/dog-breeds/golden-retriever/golden-retriever-V1R00.{vectorcraft,svg}`,
  proof `work/out/golden-retriever-V1R00@2x.png` (`check_rim.py`: clean)
- Applies to: dog-breeds milestone cards; seated frontal poses
- Status: pending

### Evidence

Two loops. Draft 1 let the cream chest bib run to y772 — the front legs
ended up as 80pt stubs underneath it and the dog read legless, propped
like a pillow. Fix: end the bib at ~y744 and run the forelegs as two
straight gold columns flanking it from ~y640 to the paws. Instant "seated
dog" read. Lesson: in a frontal sit, the foreleg columns flanking the
bib are the pose — the chest feathering is the decoration between them.

What works: the warm golds over the dark bronze disc (the darkest plate
in the series so far — lets the pale coat glow), feathered ears with
tick strokes off the bottoms, soft level mouth on a cream muzzle, folded
haunches with the hind paws peeking at the floor, feathered tail curled
around the right hip, heather tufts on the crest. Kind level-brow eyes —
the "soft-mouthed gentle expression" read.

### Proposed change

For the grammar note: *in frontal seated poses, forelegs are full-length
columns beside the chest marking — a bib that reaches the paws turns the
dog into a pillow.*
