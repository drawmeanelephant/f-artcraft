## red-eyed-tree-frog-V2R00 — flash grammar: grip is sold by pads straddling the perch, not by toe count

- Kind: lesson / analysis
- Source: issue #1361, driver `work/animals/draw_red_eyed_tree_frog_v2r00.py`,
  native/proof `work/out/red-eyed-tree-frog-V2R00.{vectorcraft,svg,pdf,@2x.png}`
- Applies to: tattoo-flash renditions; any clinging/perching subject
- Status: pending

### Evidence

First pass drew feet as many thin toes radiating long distances from the
wrist — the proof read as bare sticks ending in dots, floating off the
perch. Fix that worked: thick stroked limbs (ink pass +8pt under a green
pass) reaching to a wrist ON the stalk, then three short toes whose pads
land on BOTH sides of the stem — the straddle is what reads as a grip.
Leaf under the subject must be a clearly different value than the body or
the silhouette merges; dark leaf + light rib works. End a vertical prop
behind another element (stalk tucked behind the banner) rather than
letting it poke out beneath. `Phosphate` renders an authentic flash-inline
display face and is on the box; keep it for banner lettering. Toes at
9pt ink / 4.5pt skin satisfy the >=3pt essential-detail floor.

### Proposed change

For a future flash-rendition prompt entry: feet and hands must visibly
close around their perch — pads or fingers on both sides of the object,
limbs drawn as weighted members (ink pass then colour pass), never bare
single strokes to distant pads.
