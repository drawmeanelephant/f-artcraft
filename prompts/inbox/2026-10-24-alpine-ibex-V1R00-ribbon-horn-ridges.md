## Alpine ibex V1R00 — ribbed horns from a ribbon plus cross-ticks

- Kind: lesson
- Source: issue #1099; `work/animals/draw_alpine_ibex.py`,
  `art/animals/alpine-ibex/alpine-ibex-V1R00.*`
- Applies to: field plates, ungulates with ridged/ringed horns
- Status: pending

### Evidence

The scimitar horn worked first try: one tapered ribbon along a quadratic
arc (`quad` + `ribbon` helpers), with the transverse ridges drawn as short
strokes computed perpendicular to the same curve's tangent — the ribbing
stays registered to the arc for free. Far horn = same ribbon offset and
darkened, drawn first; it reads as depth without clutter. The floating
slab (tapered ends inside the disc, strata + cracks + salt flecks on the
crest) sells the dam-wall ledge without needing a drawn wall: the void
below does the vertigo. Tiny cousin on a higher shelf gave scale for four
shapes.

### Proposed change

For ringed horns (ibex, addax, markhor) drive the ridge ticks off the same
parameter `t` as the ribbon spine — never hand-place them. A ledge that
tapers to points inside the disc reads as exposure better than one closed
on the arc. Destination: the field-plate rules, animal section.
