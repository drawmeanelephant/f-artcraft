## Paper lantern V1R00 — a light plate in a dark-frame series

- Kind: lesson
- Source: #1232, `work/furniture/draw_paper_lantern.py`, `art/furniture/paper-lantern/`
- Applies to: field plates — the disc need not always be dark
- Status: pending

### Evidence

"Rice-paper plate" called for a light disc: rad #EBDDB6 -> #7A6440 against
the same near-black backdrop works — the disc still reads as a plate because
the rim stroke and the dark surround do the framing. The captions sit below
the disc on the backdrop, so they stay light-on-dark even on a pale plate.
Rib cage recipe that worked: body silhouette sampled with a small radial
pillow between rib rows (1+0.022*band^2) so the paper bulges like it does
between hoops; ribs drawn only as front-facing arcs bowing down
(w*0.10 sag), darker toward the edge. Hanging pieces still ground: a soft
cast shadow on the floor chord offset below does it without contact. Warm
bokeh dots at ~20 opacity read as far-off festival lights on the pale disc.

### Proposed change

Worth a line in the furniture notes: light-toned plates are in-spec —
keep the dark backdrop and light captions; pillow the silhouette between
rib/strap rows for any ribbed or wrapped skin (lanterns, basketry, cane).
