## Coffee table V1R00 — a prop or two earns the silhouette's low profile

- Kind: lesson
- Source: issue #1163, `work/furniture/draw_coffee_table.py`, `art/furniture/coffee-table/coffee-table-V1R00.*`
- Applies to: field plates — furniture
- Status: pending

### Evidence

A bare low table reads ambiguous — bench, sideboard, TV stand. One mug
with a steam wisp and a two-magazine stack on the top pin the scale
instantly: the props are the stage directions. Brass ferrule caps on the
splayed peg legs carry the mid-century code better than any extra joinery.
Rear legs at ~60% value behind the shelf barely register at @2x — that's
fine; the eye only needs the front pair plus a hint of depth. A flat
elliptical rug under the contact shadow grounds the piece without
touching the rim.

### Proposed change

Low/flat furniture may carry a single scale-giving prop (mug, book stack,
lamp glow) when the brief calls the piece a "stage" — one or two items,
fully inside the silhouette, never more.
