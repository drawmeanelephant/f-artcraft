## Moray eel V1R00 — framing masses and the honest gape

- Kind: analysis
- Source: issue #1051; `work/animals/draw_moray_eel.py`, `art/animals/moray-eel/moray-eel-V1R00.*`
- Applies to: animals field plates; rock/reef scenery; serpentine poses
- Status: pending

### Evidence

Proof `work/out/moray-eel-V1R00@2x.png`. The crevice composition holds: two
rock masses lean in from the rim and the black gap between them is the
door the eel comes through. The gape reads by its edges — pale lip-light
strokes on both jaw lines plus ~9 needle-teeth triangles — and the mouth
interior stays a single dark shape; do not draw individual fangs big.
The cleaner wrasse at the jaw corner is two filled paths + a stripe and it
carries the whole "holding station, not striking" story, which is what a
non-attack gape needs.

Working notes: paired rock masses read as WINGS or fins if their edges are
too smooth and too mirrored — what fixed it was strata cracks (5 broad
strokes, opacity ~50) plus pocks and algae fuzz, NOT reshaping. A ledge
prop was cut: anything small floating next to the head reads as an
appendage. The eel silhouette is one path — outer edge up, jaw excursion,
inner edge back down — so the gape is built INTO the outline rather than
a separate jaw piece; the throat patch then sits inside. Continuous
dorsal fin = one fat translucent stroke along the outer curve.

### Proposed change

For `prompts/series/20-field-plates.md`: mirror-flanking masses (rocks,
trunks, jaws of a scene) need texture asymmetry to stop reading as a
frame-decoration — strata + pocks before you touch geometry. And: a held
gape wants a reason — cleaner, prey, breath — one small prop beside the
mouth converts "mouth left open by mistake" into behaviour.
