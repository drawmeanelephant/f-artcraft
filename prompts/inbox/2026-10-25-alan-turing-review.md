## Alan Turing V1R00 — a desk is two chord bands, not one

- Kind: analysis
- Source: issue #1552; `work/scientists/draw_alan_turing.py`,
  `art/scientists/alan-turing/alan-turing-V1R00.{vectorcraft,svg}`,
  proof `work/out/alan-turing-V1R00@2x.png` (`check_rim.py`: clean,
  first pass)
- Applies to: scientist milestone cards; furniture/prop grounds
- Status: pending

### Evidence

Shipped in one loop; the pieces that carried it:

- **Desk = slab + wedge.** A narrow chord slab (top face seen edge-on,
  two curved rails `y1`/`y2` ~28pt apart) reads as the desktop; a second
  wedge from the lower rail closing on the disc's `A420` arc reads as
  the under-desk shadow. Two shapes, both rim-safe, and the figure's
  torso simply disappears behind them — no need to crop the suit to the
  desk line.
- **Punched tape = a band path + parametric marks.** Each tape run is
  one filled band (`p0->p1` cubic offset ±11pt) and its punch dots are
  dots/squares interpolated along the same segment — tape and marks can
  never drift apart.
- **The reel sells the tape.** Wound concentric rings + a brass hub on a
  strut reads instantly as a spool; the tape exits at a tangent, not the
  centre.
- **Background machines at 8–16% opacity**: two rounded cabinet
  outlines, three dials, five indicator dots. Enough to say "machine
  room" without drawing a Bombe.
- **Young/clean-shaven reads as cheek fullness + no facial hair** —
  fuller face oval, side-parted short crop, irises dropped toward the
  work. Distinct enough from Tesla's hollow cheeks and moustache.
- Milestone `TURING MACHINE · 1936` in Source Sans 3, tracking 480 →
  443pt wide, 65pt clear of the rim.

### Proposed change

For the milestone grammar: *a desk/table/bench for a seated figure is a
two-band chord construction (surface slab + under-shadow wedge on the
arc); props that feed through hands share one parametric run so marks,
edges and grips stay registered.* Destination: the scientist/milestone
card note at the next consolidation.
