## Bactrian camel V1R00 — the two humps ARE the silhouette

- Kind: analysis
- Source: issue #1101; `work/animals/draw_bactrian_camel.py`,
  `art/animals/bactrian-camel/bactrian-camel-V1R00.*`
- Applies to: field plates, camels, silhouette-first design
- Status: pending

### Evidence

First pass survived critique. The Bactrian reads instantly because the
topline does the work: two separate peaked humps with a saddle dip, then a
long S-neck rising off the chest — draw those three arcs honestly and the
species can't be anything else. Small head, nostril slit, and the
double-row lashes (upper AND lower lash fans on the same eye) reward the
@2x look without cluttering 1x. The minus-forty mood came from props, not
palette tricks: a breath ellipse column off the muzzle, snow patches on the
arc-closed sand floor, wind streaks. The distant caravan — body bump + two
hump bumps + neck tick + leg ticks at ~20px — reads as camels because it
reuses the same silhouette recipe at miniature scale.

### Proposed change

For silhouette-signature animals, build the far-line miniatures from the
same structural recipe as the hero — same hump count, same neck gesture —
not generic blobs. Destination: the field-plate rules, background-staffage
note.
