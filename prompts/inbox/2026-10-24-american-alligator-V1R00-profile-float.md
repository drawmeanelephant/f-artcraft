## American alligator V1R00 — crocodylians are profile animals

- Kind: lesson
- Source: issue #1074, `work/animals/draw_american_alligator.py`,
  `art/animals/american-alligator/american-alligator-V1R00.*`
- Applies to: field plates, animals
- Status: pending

### Evidence

First pass tried a head-on ambush float (two raised orbits, broad snout
toward the viewer): it proofed as a cartoon hippo — stacked domes, no
snout read. The redraw went to strict profile: long flat U-snout left,
nostril dome raised at the tip, amber eye on a raised orbit, the jaw
seam running the full snout and tipping up at the hinge, a few upper
tooth nubs overhanging the seam (lower teeth tucked inside — the
alligator/crocodile tell), then a low scute ridge trailing right and
sinking through the waterline. Reads instantly. Cypress knees were
drawn before the water band so their bases submerge, with reflection
streaks and ripple rings tying them into the surface.

### Proposed change

For `prompts/series/20-field-plates.md` known traps: an animal whose
species code lives in its silhouette (gator vs croc vs hippo) fails
head-on even when every part is correct — default to the profile when
the jaw/snout line is the identifier, and let the waterline carry the
"float" instead of stacking raised features.
