## Border collie V1R00 — the working crouch reads once the ears stop being horns

- Kind: analysis
- Source: issue #1498; `work/dog-breeds/draw_border_collie.py`,
  `art/dog-breeds/border-collie/border-collie-V1R00.{vectorcraft,svg}`,
  proof `work/out/border-collie-V1R00@2x.png` (`check_rim.py`: clean)
- Applies to: dog-breeds milestone cards; quadruped heads and ears generally
- Status: pending

### Evidence

Three render-and-critique loops to a card that survives critique.

Draft 1 failed two ways. Ears drawn as tall two-part tubes read as
rabbit/donkey ears and owned the whole face. The head was also tipped
~70° nose-down, so the "fixed gaze" read as the dog inspecting its paws.
Draft 2 kept the head frontal — skull broad between the ears tapering to
the muzzle, one white shape for blaze+muzzle, eyes flanking it — and the
collie stare arrived immediately. But the ear fold flaps pointed
up-and-*out* past the base tips and read as little horns. Final: fold
flaps hinged inward-down inside the base silhouette — semi-pricked at a
glance.

What works in the shipped card: the low stalking silhouette (back level,
belly near the peat floor), the amber stare under flattened brows, white
bib/blaze/socks/tail-tip as overlay shapes, heather tufts and mist
sitting on a floor that closes on the disc arc, caption stack clearing
the rim by ~69pt.

What could still improve in a future R: the chest bib edges read
slightly as a white pouch where it overlaps the foreleg tops; the hind
legs are indistinct dark mass — acceptable in a crouch but a clearer
hock line would help; the L ear fold still has a hint of "little cap."

### Proposed change

None needed for the series grammar — the conventions are in
`2026-10-24-milestone-cards-grammar.md`. Lesson worth folding into the
field-plates traps at consolidation: *frontal head + profile body is a
legal pose when the brief demands eye contact; ear fold flaps must land
inside the ear-base silhouette, never outside it.*
