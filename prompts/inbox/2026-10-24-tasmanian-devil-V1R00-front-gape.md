## tasmanian-devil V1R00 — the gape carries a front-facing plate

- Kind: lesson
- Source: #1038, work/animals/draw_tasmanian_devil.py, art/animals/tasmanian-devil/tasmanian-devil-V1R00.*
- Applies to: animals field plates; any subject whose signature is the open mouth
- Status: pending

### Evidence

Front-on crouch with the mouth thrown to ~60% of head height reads instantly:
shut-eye strokes, teeth hooked from both jaw rims, tongue low in the floor,
whiskers flaring past the cheeks. The white chest crescent sits below the
chin where a real devil wears it. Loose tail shapes floating past the flank
read as dropped props — the tail only read once its base went under the body
silhouette (drawn before the body, emerging from behind the flank).

### Proposed change

For `prompts/series/20-field-plates.md` known traps: appendage bases belong
under the body silhouette — a tail drawn after the body with a visible root
reads as a separate object.
