# leatherback-sea-turtle-V1R00 — attachments at a silhouette tip read as antennae

- Kind: lesson
- Source: issue #1113, driver `work/animals/draw_leatherback_sea_turtle.py`,
  native + proof `art/animals/leatherback-sea-turtle/`
- Applies to: animal field plates; silhouette-driven subjects
- Status: pending

### Evidence

Open-ocean plate, no floor — the depth vignette closes on the disc's own
`A420,420` arc like a floor would. The subject is built on a rotated
shell axis (`P(s,t)` = centre + u·s + n·t) so keels, mottle and outline
all stay registered to the dive angle.

What failed twice: any separate small blade attached at the shell's rear
tip — two hind-flipper fans, then a single paddle, then a "tail nub" —
each read as an ear or antenna on the proof, not anatomy. The silhouette
wants a clean taper; trailing limbs behind a point are unrecoverable at
plate scale and were cut.

What survived: the pale throat patch reads as a mouth whenever it
reaches the snout — it must stay under the jaw and below ~35% opacity.
Seven keels work as tapered strokes keyed to the shell axis, but the two
outermost needed opacity ~38 or the dome reads striped, not keeled.

### Proposed change

For `prompts/core/` consideration: at silhouette tips and corners,
subtract before you add — an ambiguous protrusion costs more than the
omitted detail. Pale patches on dark animals should never touch a
silhouette edge that could read as a mouth or eye surround.
