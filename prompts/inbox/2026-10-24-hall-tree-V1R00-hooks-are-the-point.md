## Hall tree V1R00 — on a coat rack the hooks carry the drawing

- Kind: lesson
- Source: issue #1224, `work/furniture/draw_hall_tree.py`,
  `art/furniture/hall-tree/hall-tree-V1R00.{vectorcraft,svg,@2x.png}`
- Applies to: field plates — furniture (hallstands, coat racks, anything hung)
- Status: pending

### Evidence

First proof failed the subject: hooks drawn as mount + two thin stroked
curls (4pt) read as dark nubs — the coat rack had no coat rack in it.
Fixed by drawing each hook as mount plate + two screw dots + each arm as
a dark under-stroke (sw+1.4) with a lit iron stroke inside it + a ball
tip ellipse; arms sweep ~3.5in past the post edge so they silhouette
against the backdrop, not just the oak. Painter's-order trap caught in
review: hooks must be drawn AFTER the near-post prism or the post face
covers them. Mud-season props that worked: damp olive coat hanging off
the far top hook (one smooth silhouette + collar + 3 fold strokes + a
wide wet-hem stroke), two L-silhouette boots on the drip tray (shaft +
foot + sole line), a leaning furled umbrella (tapered quad + ferrule +
J-handle). Mirror reads with a pale glass ramp, a faint reflected
doorway quad and one diagonal sheen — keep the glass darker than the
frame's lit oak or the pale slab swallows the piece.

### Proposed change

Furniture spec candidate: "Hung hardware (hooks, pulls, ring drops) must
overhang its mount and be double-stroked dark+lit — a stroke flush to
the surface disappears. On multi-part pieces, draw hanging props on the
far side and keep the near face clear." Pending.
