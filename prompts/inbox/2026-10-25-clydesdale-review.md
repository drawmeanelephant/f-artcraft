## Clydesdale V1R00 — feather is a skirt + strands, and stockings are masks

- Kind: analysis
- Source: issue #1516; `work/horse-breeds/draw_clydesdale.py`,
  `art/horse-breeds/clydesdale/clydesdale-V1R00.{vectorcraft,svg}`,
  proof `work/out/clydesdale-V1R00@2x.png`
- Applies to: milestone cards, horse breeds, marked/feathered quadrupeds
- Status: pending

### Evidence

Shipped on the second render. Standing square on the single-silhouette
recipe: a deeper barrel, thicker crest and heavier jaw than the arabian
carry the draught mass. The breed code is markings: one broad white
blaze overlay covering the face front from brow to muzzle, four white
stocking masks over each cannon, and the feather done as a cream
bell-skirt fill that overhangs the hoof plus four wavy strand strokes
(3.5pt) per leg — the strands read as layered hair, the skirt gives
them something to hang from. Hooves survive as small dark crescents
under each skirt. Offside stockings at 85% opacity keep the near/far
hierarchy without inventing a second white.

Survives critique as V1R00 with residue: the mass reads slightly
capybara — the shoulder could stand to drop the chest floor a few
points and the head is a touch roman-nosed where the brief's type is
closer to straight. The far-side legs almost vanish behind the near
stockings; a 4–6pt bigger offset would help the 3/4 read.

### Proposed change

For the horse-breeds/milestone note at consolidation: *leg markings are
white mask fills drawn over the leg silhouette, NOT the leg silhouette
itself — draw the dark column first, then lay the stocking on it, then
feather over the stocking's lower edge.* And: *feather = filled bell
skirt + a few wavy strands ≥3pt; strands alone read as fringe, skirt
alone reads as a boot.*
