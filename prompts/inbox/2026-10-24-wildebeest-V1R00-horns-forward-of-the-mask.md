## Wildebeest V1R00 — gnu horns must sit forward of the face mask

- Kind: lesson
- Source: issue #1098; `work/animals/draw_wildebeest.py`,
  `art/animals/wildebeest/wildebeest-V1R00.*`
- Applies to: field plates, profile ungulates (horns, masks)
- Status: pending

### Evidence

First proof read as a grey potato: head too small, and the horn crescent
vanished because the face mask covered the whole cheek — dark horn on a
dark mask. Two moves fixed it: shrinking the mask to the muzzle only so the
crescent reads against the tan cheek, and running BOTH horns down the front
of the face as thick crescents (the far one a darker echo ~15px behind)
rather than tucking the far horn behind the poll where the mane eats it.
The boss wants to be a low knobby band along the poll, not a blob — a blob
reads as a hat. Pale beard also had to hug the jawline; as a detached
triangle it rendered as a bib.

### Proposed change

For profile ungulates: keep any dark face mask to the muzzle third so horns
read against the cheek; draw near+far horns as overlapping forward
crescents; build the boss as a band. Destination: the animal section of the
field-plate rules.
