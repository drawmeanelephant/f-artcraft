## joni-mitchell V1R00 — arms must reach their hands, not stop short

- Kind: lesson / analysis
- Source: issue #1541; `work/musicians/draw_joni_mitchell.py`,
  `art/musicians/joni-mitchell/joni-mitchell-V1R00.{vectorcraft,svg}`,
  proof `work/out/joni-mitchell-V1R00@2x.png`
- Applies to: milestone cards / any figure holding a prop
- Status: pending

### Evidence

Two render-and-critique loops:

1. First pass had both forearm sleeves ending mid-air with the hands
   drawn as small round blobs floating at the prop — the fret hand read
   as a knob stuck on the dulcimer headstock, the strum hand as a ball
   glued to the body. Fix: sleeves reshaped to actually reach the hands
   (sleeve cuff overlaps the wrist, hand overlaps the instrument), and
   hands drawn as mitts wider than the wrist, not circles.
2. Centre-parted hair works as two long curtain shapes over a back mass
   + one dark seam at the part; the curtains must hug the face edge or
   they read as a hood. Heart soundholes (a real feature of her
   dulcimer) carry the instrument read at card scale better than a
   soundhole circle would.

`check_rim` clean; `BLUE · 1971` 196pt wide, clears the arc by ~65pt.

### Proposed change

For held props: always draw the limb reaching INTO the hand shape —
an arm that stops short and a floating hand is the single fastest way a
card reads wrong at proof scale. Destination: milestone-cards note in
`prompts/series/` at next consolidation.
