## Indian peafowl V1R00 — front-on display reads at plate scale

- Kind: lesson
- Source: issue #1118, `work/animals/draw_indian_peafowl.py`,
  `art/animals/indian-peafowl/indian-peafowl-V1R00.{vectorcraft,svg,@2x.png}`
- Applies to: animals field plates (fan/display compositions)
- Status: pending

### Evidence

First pass put a squat blue column under the fan: no neck, pale head read
as a "mask", crest vanished into the fan quills. Rework within V1R00 gave
the body a narrow neck flaring into the breast, deepened the head to
royal blue, enlarged the crest fanlets and thickened legs to 5pt. The
proof now reads instantly as a displaying cock: 14 covert ribbons carry
one ocellus ring at the tips plus two sparse inner rings; the eye reads
at four layers (gold halo / dark ring / turquoise heart / navy pupil).
Front view is the right call for a display piece — the ocelli all face
the viewer, which is what the real train does.

### Proposed change

For display-fan birds (peafowl, argus, birds-of-paradise): draw the fan
as a silhouette field + tapered covert ribbons + ocelli rings, and give
the body a distinct neck column — a uniform-width body reads as a
penguin. Candidate addition to the animals section of
`prompts/series/20-field-plates.md`.
