## Miles Davis V1R00 — "half-shadowed" is one translucent wedge, not more ink

- Kind: analysis
- Source: issue #1534; `work/musicians/draw_miles_davis.py`,
  `art/musicians/miles-davis/miles-davis-V1R00.{vectorcraft,svg}`,
  proof `work/out/miles-davis-V1R00@2x.png`
- Applies to: musicians milestone series; moody low-key cards
- Status: pending

### Evidence

One render pass held up; this was the quietest card and the grammar
carried it.

- **Half-shadow face:** a single closed shape over the face's right half
  at ~42% plate-hued darkness (`#12161F`), drawn after the features,
  with a matching 30% wedge on the same-side shoulder. The head stays
  one skin gradient underneath — simpler and reads harder than per-side
  skin ramps.
- **"Nothing grins" is a vocabulary, not an absence:** level 4.5pt mouth
  line (no upward curl), half-lidded eyes as two strokes each — a hood
  arc + a smaller eye line beneath it — plus a faint pencil-moustache
  hint. The face reads "unimpressed" not "blank".
- **A horn held low works at hip level angled ~45°** — grip at the valve
  casing, bell flaring outward. Reads "at rest", contrasts the raised
  Armstrong horn (same part list, mirrored attitude).
- **Understatement needs ONE cue per layer:** pocket square + narrow
  tie + slim lapels for "sharp suit"; a lone music-stand silhouette for
  "bandstand". The cool steel plate (#70809A→#202A3A) does the rest —
  resist stacking more props.
- The stand silhouette at far left is borderline — reads as dark
  furniture more than a stand at card scale; a next revision could
  delete it without loss.

### Proposed change

For the musicians note: low-key cards take a single translucent shade
wedge across face AND same-side shoulder (the shadow should feel like it
falls, not like makeup); expression restraint = short level strokes, not
fewer features.
