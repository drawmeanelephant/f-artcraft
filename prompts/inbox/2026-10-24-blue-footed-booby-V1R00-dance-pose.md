- Applies to: field plates / animals

### Evidence

The courtship sky-point carries the plate: head thrown up, long bill open
to the sky, one turquoise foot lifted and splayed toward the viewer while
the other plants on a basalt chord closing on the disc arc. What worked:
the raised display foot reads instantly once the web is drawn as three
splayed lobes with toe creases, and a narrow cocked tail wedge stays
legible next to the folded wing as long as it is slimmer and points a
beat higher than the wingtip. What failed on pass one: a short blunt wing
blob and a downward tail wedge that read as two competing wings — fixed
by lengthening the wing down the flank and cocking the tail up. The 58pt
`BLUE-FOOTED BOOBY` name (806pt) clears the rim by only ~2.6pt; a longer
name at this size would not.

### Proposed change

Note for `prompts/series/20-field-plates.md`: for bird plates, a raised
display foot wants the web drawn as splayed lobes plus a pale glint — a
plain mitten reads as a fist. Names >15 chars at 58pt should be proofed
against the rim arc early; consider ~54pt for the longest names.
