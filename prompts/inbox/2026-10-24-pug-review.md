## Pug V1R00 — front-facing bust: the mask is a shape, the wrinkles are strokes

- Source: issue #1505; `work/dog-breeds/draw_pug.py`,

- Applies to: dog-breeds milestone series
- Status: pending

### Evidence

Shipped on the labrador bust chassis (shoulders land inside the rim,
bottom closes on the disc arc). Front-facing symmetry wanted a different
stack than the profile dogs: skull silhouette first, then the black mask
as ONE overlay shape covering muzzle + eye surrounds, then the wrinkles
as strokes on the fawn above it. That ordering keeps the eyes free of
wrinkle noise — the breed is the eyes, and two 52pt near-black discs
with hard glints carry it alone.

The double-curl tail reads best as a stroked spiral (16pt warm fawn +
6pt sheen pass, round cap) drawn BEFORE the shoulders so the bust
overlaps its root — "peeking" needs the curl interrupted, a fully drawn
spiral reads as a lollipop. Dewlap arcs across the neck/shoulder seam
hide the bust-stack junction that otherwise reads as a pawn silhouette.
Button ears are small dark smooth blobs at the skull corners; keeping
them under ~70pt tall stops them reading as a bear. check_rim clean,
milestone cleared by 69pt.

### Proposed change

For the remaining front-facing breeds: mask/markings as single overlay
shapes, wrinkles strictly above them on the base coat, eyes last and
unmarked. Fold into the milestone-cards series note at consolidation.
