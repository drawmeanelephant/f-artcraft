## Ruby-throated hummingbird V1R00 — hover needs negative space

- Kind: lesson
- Source: issue #1119, `work/animals/draw_ruby_throated_hummingbird.py`,
  `art/animals/ruby-throated-hummingbird/ruby-throated-hummingbird-V1R00.{vectorcraft,svg,@2x.png}`
- Applies to: animals field plates (flying/hovering subjects)
- Status: pending

### Evidence

The bird hangs in open plate space on purpose: the gap around it is what
sells hover. A single bloom read thin for "flower-bed", so a second
half-open corolla was added on a side spur — but only after the proof
showed it floating in space; every side shoot needs a visible spur
stroke back to the stem. Wing blur works as 3 translucent sweeps at
16–28% opacity behind one crisp wing, not as a stack of solid shapes.
The gorget survives at plate scale as a small saturated shield under the
bill — sparks on it (5pt dots at 70%) are worth the objects.

### Proposed change

For aerial subjects: anchor the pose to a stem/branch entering from the
floor band, keep ≥40% of the disc as breathing room, and give every
side element a visible connector. Candidate note for
`prompts/series/20-field-plates.md`.
