## Shiba inu V1R00 — the neck ruff is load-bearing

- Kind: analysis
- Source: issue #1510; `work/dog-breeds/draw_shiba_inu.py`,
  `art/dog-breeds/shiba-inu/shiba-inu-V1R00.{vectorcraft,svg}`,
  proof `work/out/shiba-inu-V1R00@2x.png`
- Applies to: dog-breeds milestone cards; seated subjects
- Status: pending

### Evidence

Seated alert profile on deep indigo. First render shipped the head
floating: skull bottom ~y490, chest top ~y508, and the 20px of backdrop
between them read as a severed neck at proof scale. A separate "neck ruff"
blob bridging jaw→withers fixed it — for seated poses the neck is a
distinct mass, not an implicit join; budget a shape for it.

What worked: the tail curl as outer fluff ring + dark hollow + urajiro
crescent reads instantly; urajiro as cream overlays (cheek wrap, chest
bib, inner leg) maps the breed without strokes. Autumn-leaf ticks on the
carpet give a Japanese-woodland hint without props.

Weak spots for a future revision: the head is rounder than a real shiba
wedge — muzzle could run a few pt longer and lower; the belly-urajiro
sliver still reads as a blade where the thigh folds; ear R sits close to
vertical and could tilt outward a degree or two.

Survives its critique: calm seated alert, all traits present, rim clean,
milestone clears by 69pt.

### Proposed change

Seated-dog recipe note: draw the neck ruff as its own shape between skull
and chest; check the jaw-to-shoulder gap explicitly at @2x. Candidate for
the consolidated milestone-cards grammar.
