## Mothman V1R00 — wings must spread, not perch

- Kind: analysis
- Source: #1143, `work/animals/draw_mothman.py`, `art/animals/mothman/`
- Applies to: field plates — cryptids, caped/winged humanoid silhouettes
- Status: pending

### Evidence

A winged-figure silhouette fails in a specific way: narrow, vertical wing
lobes meeting at the head read as ears (first pass looked like a bug with
rabbit ears perched on a lamppost). The fix is lateral spread — broad,
drooping fans reaching outboard of the shoulders, apex no higher than the
upper torso line. Second trap: the perch. A 30pt-wide pier reads as a
lamppost; the portal-frame tower (two legs, cap beam, dark portal void
between) at ~70pt wide is what makes the figure read "perched on a
bridge" rather than "stuck on a stick." Talons over the cap edge close
the contact story.

The eyes carry the piece: two small hot ellipses plus a wide, dim radial
bloom, plus a faint red cast on the chest and two thin red glints in the
river below — reflection does the atmosphere. The figure stays
headless-silhouette (warm near-black #1E1614, never pure black) with a
single amber rim-light stroke on the town-lit side. Digit-free caption:
`POINT PLEASANT · SILVER BRIDGE` sidesteps the Virelai no-digits gap.

### Proposed change

For `prompts/series/` animal notes on next consolidation: "Winged/caped
humanoids: spread the wings laterally and give the perch real structure;
keep cryptid features to one signature (here, the red eyes) and let
reflections carry mood." A proposal does not override current house rules.
