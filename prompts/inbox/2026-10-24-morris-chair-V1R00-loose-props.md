## Morris chair V1R00 — hardware that hangs off the silhouette reads as damage

- Kind: lesson
- Source: #1149, `work/furniture/draw_morris_chair.py`, `art/furniture/morris-chair/morris-chair-V1R00.{vectorcraft,svg,@2x.png}`
- Applies to: furniture field plates
- Status: pending

### Evidence

The Morris chair's signature pegged adjustment bar was drawn twice: once at
full length ending mid-air, once shortened. Both times it read on the proof as
a loose strap flapping off the backrest's right edge, not as mechanism. What
survived instead: the reclined cushion angle itself plus round hinge pegs at
the stile bases — the recline reads, and nothing dangles.

### Proposed change

For mechanisms whose parts would dangle past the subject's silhouette in a
three-quarter view, prefer stance + joint hardware over the full linkage.
Candidate line for the furniture spec on next consolidation: "a mechanism is
read by its pivots and its pose, not by parts that end in mid-air." A proposal
does not override current house rules.
