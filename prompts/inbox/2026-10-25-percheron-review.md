## Percheron V1R00 — the draft walk wants a low head, not an arched neck

- Kind: analysis
- Source: issue #1525; `work/horse-breeds/draw_percheron.py`,
  `art/horse-breeds/percheron/percheron-V1R00.{vectorcraft,svg}`,
  proof `work/out/percheron-V1R00@2x.png`
- Applies to: horse-breeds milestone cards; draft/working poses
- Status: pending

### Evidence

Shipped after two render-and-critique loops. First pass reused the
andalusian's arched baroque carriage and read as a generic light horse:
the "working walk" only landed once the head carriage dropped ~30pt and
reached forward — the neck top running as one near-straight diagonal
from poll to withers reads as work, the arch reads as school. Draft
mass is the belgian recipe held: barrel to y~640, leg tops ~44–48pt
wide, hooves 14–16pt half-width.

Two breed tells confirmed:

- Clean legs are a POSITIVE instruction, not an absence — the brief
  makes "unfeathered" the tell versus the clydesdale, so resisting the
  feather reflex is the drawing.
- Little ears on a big plain head beat any other single detail for
  "draft".

What failed and got fixed: a hogged mane drawn as a filled band
strictly inside the crest edge read as a dark PATCH on the neck, not
hair. The fix is the same scalloped-hem construction as the andalusian
mane, just shorter — let the band sit ON the crest silhouette with
small hem scallops and it reads as a tidy mane.

Survives critique as V1R00 with residue: the far fore is almost
swallowed behind the reaching near fore (a 6–8pt bigger offset would
help); the neck base could carry ~10pt more thickness into the chest.

### Proposed change

For the horse-breeds card note at consolidation: *working/draft
carriage = head lowered and reaching forward, crest as a near-straight
diagonal; arched vertical carriage is reserved for baroque/school
poses.* And: *short manes still want the scalloped-hem crest band —
a band floating inside the neck edge reads as a stain.*
