## jaguar-V2R00 — rosettes as broken neon rings; flat-bottomed busts read as tabletops

- Kind: lesson / analysis
- Source: issue #1360, driver `work/animals/draw_jaguar_v2r00.py`,
  native/proof `work/out/jaguar-V2R00.{vectorcraft,svg,pdf,@2x.png}`
- Applies to: psychedelic rendition series; bust-style compositions generally
- Status: pending

### Evidence

Translating jaguar rosettes into two broken concentric arcs (235° sweeps,
staggered start angles, hot yellow core) keeps the "radiating rings" brief
without turning the cat leopard-uniform — vary ring radius 13–18pt and
rotate the break per rosette. The 3/4 read comes from the muzzle pad
drifting ~20pt left of the skull centreline plus a narrower far eye; ears
alone don't sell it. First pass ended the chest and shoulder paths on a
straight y=830 edge: the proof read as a cat behind a counter. Closing the
bust with a shallow downward curve (bottom control points ~860–884) lets it
float in the colour field. Amber eyes stay legible when pupil fill is near-
black violet and the "eye halo" arcs are kept behind the brow folds at
~50% opacity.

### Proposed change

For the psychedelic-rendition note set: never close a subject bust on a
horizontal edge — a flat cut reads as furniture, not field. Round the
closing curve at least 40pt below the widest part of the mass.
