# Kagu V1R00 — a crest of stroked feathers survives

- Kind: lesson
- Source: issue #1133; `work/animals/draw_kagu.py`,
  `art/animals/kagu/kagu-V1R00.{vectorcraft,svg,@2x.png}`
- Applies to: animals field plates; crests, plumes, display feathers
- Status: pending

### Evidence

Proof `work/out/kagu-V1R00@2x.png`. The partly raised crest was drawn
as five stroked quads (9pt body stroke + 3.5pt shaft overlay) fanning
-8°..-80° from one crown point. At ≥9pt a stroked feather keeps its
read at @2x — no silhouette fill needed, unlike antlers (cf. the
wapiti note). Key was keeping the fan loose and back-swept, not a
full semicircle, and letting each feather share one base so the crest
grows from the crown.

Folded wing stayed plain per the brief — a closed translucent wing
shape, no barred display pattern. Red-orange legs at 9–10pt ribbons
hold mass; toes 4.5pt fanning three-forward one-back read as a
grounded foot, not a wader's.

### Proposed change

For `prompts/series/20-field-plates.md`: stroked feathers are viable
at ≥9pt when they share a visible base; below that they need filled
leaf shapes. Crests read better as a tight angular fan than a full
radial burst.
