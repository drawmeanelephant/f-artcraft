## Greyhound V1R00 — draw the neck under the legs at full stretch

- Kind: analysis
- Source: issue #1513; `work/dog-breeds/draw_greyhound.py`,
  `art/dog-breeds/greyhound/greyhound-V1R00.{vectorcraft,svg}`,
  proof `work/out/greyhound-V1R00@2x.png`
- Applies to: dog-breeds milestone cards; airborne/gallop poses
- Status: pending

### Evidence

Double-suspension gallop on a teal-slate disc — all four feet off the
turf, front pair reaching, hind pair driving back. The aerodynamic line
held: deep brisket, the waist tuck sweeping up to the loin, long whip
tail tapering to a point, rose ears folded flat as small back-pointing
triangles.

Lesson: at full stretch the neck must be drawn *before* the front legs.
Drawn after them, its lower-right edge stroked a visible crease across
the shoulder where the reaching legs overlap it. Under the legs, only
the throat line shows — which is the correct anatomy read.

Airborne poses want the shadow shallow and low-contrast (26% here vs 32%
for standing dogs) and a beat of air between paws and crest. Flattened
horizontal grass ticks on the floor carry the pace without speed lines.

Weak spots: hind legs + tail cluster three parallel strokes at the rump
— a revision could splay the far hind further from the tail; the rose
ears are almost too small to read at 1x but hold at @2x.

Survives its critique: unmistakable sprint silhouette, rim clean,
milestone clears by 69pt.

### Proposed change

Gallop rule for the grammar: neck before legs when both front legs reach
past the throat line; airborne dogs take a lighter, lower shadow.
Candidate for consolidation.
