## Filing cabinet V1R00 — office steel wants hardware, not ornament

- Kind: lesson
- Source: issue #1222, `work/furniture/draw_filing_cabinet.py`,
  `art/furniture/filing-cabinet/filing-cabinet-V1R00.{vectorcraft,svg,@2x.png}`
- Applies to: field plates — furniture (metal case pieces)
- Status: pending

### Evidence

A letter file is a plain box; the subject reads through its hardware
rhythm, not its silhouette. What held at plate scale: four identical
drawer fronts drawn as proud prisms (0.55in) so each carries its own
inked edge and face gradient; a three-part pull per drawer (dark recess
slot, nickel lip strip, thin glint line — the recess alone read as a
dead black rectangle in the first proof); a nickel card frame with a
manila card and two ruled lines sells "alphabetical certainty" without
letterforms. Cool grey-green enamel ramp (#202726→#7E8B84) plus a broad
low-opacity sheen quad on the side face keeps the big steel slab from
going flat. Office mood props that survived: faint venetian streak
bands, pale cool light pool. Key bezel is a 3-shape cluster (radial
bezel + dark slot) — enough.

### Proposed change

Furniture spec candidate: "On metal case pieces the hardware IS the
drawing: draw every drawer pull as recess + lip + glint (three shapes),
never as a bare dark slot; card frames get frame + card + ruled lines."
