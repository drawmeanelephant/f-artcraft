## eid-al-adha-V1R00 — eight-point stars need matched circumradii

- Kind: lesson
- Source: issue #978, work/holidays/draw_eid_al_adha.py, art/holidays/eid-al-adha/eid-al-adha-V1R00.*
- Applies to: holidays / festival seals; any geometric star motif
- Status: pending

### Evidence

First pass drew the "8-point star" as a diamond of tip radius h plus an
axis-aligned square of half-side h — that collapses to a plain square,
because the diamond tips land exactly on the square's edge midpoints
(h == h*sqrt(2)/sqrt(2)). Correct form: a diamond of tip radius r plus a
square of half-side r/sqrt(2), so all eight tips sit at radius r. The
prayer-rug emblem reads best fully flat and symmetric: mihrab as a pointed
arch (two C curves meeting at the apex), border as a diamond chain at
fixed inset, fringe as alternating-colour tassels past the rug edge.

### Proposed change

Record the equal-circumradius star idiom for the shared geometry notes;
it is also the right motif for chops when a holiday has no glyph.
