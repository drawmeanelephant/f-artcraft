## Isaac Newton V1R00 — periwig = smooth mass + C-curve ringlets; side-table props work

- Kind: analysis
- Source: issue #1547; `work/scientists/draw_isaac_newton.py`,
  `art/scientists/isaac-newton/isaac-newton-V1R00.{vectorcraft,svg}`,

- Applies to: scientist milestone cards; period wigs and small props
- Status: pending

### Evidence

One loop — the composition worked on the first render.

- **The baroque wig is three layers:** a smooth back mass (crown + two
  falls flanking the face to the shoulders), a centre-part stroke at the
  crown, then a dozen `c`-relative C-curve ringlets at ~45% opacity plus
  two darker groove strokes between the falls. Texture strokes carry
  the "curl" reading — the silhouette stays smooth.
- **A side prop table reads cleanly beside the bust.** Small tabletop
  bar at y≈742–757 plus one leg, parked at x610–742 right of the robe's
  shoulder — the single apple on it is the myth-as-prop and the card's
  only warm accent. Both stay ≥35pt off the rim.
- **The prism diagram works as a ghost:** one faint incident beam, a
  small translucent triangle, three thin spectral rays at 11–14%
  opacity fanning off it. Literal and period-correct without competing
  with the face.
- Milestone `PRINCIPIA · 1687` in Source Sans 3 at tracking 520 —
  335pt wide, inside the cap, matching einstein's line treatment.

### Proposed change

For the milestone grammar: *period hair is silhouette + texture strokes
(smooth mass, part line, ringlet C-curves, dark grooves between falls) —
never a scalloped outer edge; side tables/lecterns are legal props
beside the bust if they hold ≥35pt off the rim.*
