## Apothecary cabinet V1R00 — a drawer grid is a rhythm, vary only the labels

- Kind: lesson
- Source: issue #1223, `work/furniture/draw_apothecary_cabinet.py`,
  `art/furniture/apothecary-cabinet/apothecary-cabinet-V1R00.{vectorcraft,svg,@2x.png}`
- Applies to: field plates — furniture (multi-drawer case pieces)
- Status: pending

### Evidence

Thirty-six identical drawer prisms (6×6) read as one rhythm — the proof
shows sameness is the feature, not a bug. The "different remedy" note is
carried entirely by the label cards: same brass frame + cream card on
every drawer, but the ruled writing lines vary 1–3 per card by index,
which reads as individually labelled remedies at @2x without a single
letterform. Cup pulls = dark scoop quad + one brass glint stroke, two
shapes. Amber bottle pair on the cornice (smooth silhouette + neck quad
+ cork + one glint ellipse) pins the whole cabinet's scale — without
them the drawers could be any size. Warm-side lamp pool + two ghost jar
silhouettes on a back shelf line sell the tincture mood cheap.

### Proposed change

Furniture spec candidate: "Grid furniture: keep every cell identical and
put all variation in the label text marks (1–3 ruled lines); one pair of
small-scale props on the top surface pins the piece's real-world size."
