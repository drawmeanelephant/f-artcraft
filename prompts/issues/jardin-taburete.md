# Furniture — field plate: Jardín taburete (Colombia)

## Brief

A construction study of the armless wood-and-hide chair called a
taburete in Jardín, Antioquia. Show the backrest, hide seat, joinery and
slight leather sag in a near-side three-quarter view. This is a chair
with a back, not a backless bar stool. Warm hide against a cool indigo
disc. The region also has a painted-chair tradition: do not copy an
individual artisan's painting or invent generic Indigenous ornament.
