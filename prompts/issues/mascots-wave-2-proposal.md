# Mascots — wave 2 expansion proposal: the Cold Storage Annex

Status: draft, not posted. One leaf issue per row when approved;
`type:illustration` + `work:original`. See `.github/LABELS.md`.

Companion-plate grammar (`prompts/series/30-companion-plates.md`): same
disc and caption stack as the field plates, slug `<number>.<name>`, second
line is the mascot's office. Check `art/mascots/` and issues before
claiming; the roster there is inventory, not inspiration.

The cast so far runs three registers: the tech gremlins (003–121), the
comfort-bureaucracy spirits (201–446), and the status-code residents
(400–938). Wave 2 proposes a coherent fourth cluster — **the Cold
Storage Annex**: the spirits of dead formats and retired media, filed
rather than mourned. Twelve subjects on the unused 700 block (672 and
677 are taken; 701–712 are free). Tone follows the house register —
arch, tender, never mocking the obsolete.

Shared leaf brief: 1000×1000, dark backdrop, gradient disc (500,460,
r≈420), name line ~54–58pt at y≈930, office line ~22–24pt tracked at
y≈968, warm dark ink, gradients and translucent shading. These are
objects-with-personality, not scenes — one subject per plate, and the
format's material truth (tape gloss, card stock, carbon sheen, thermal
paper fade) does the expressive work.

Watch-items: no real brand marks; roman numerals and archive numbers are
fine, invented archive IDs are not. The thin-geometry trap is real for
chad grids, perf tape and rolodex cards — ≥3pt at plate scale, judged on
the @2x proof.
