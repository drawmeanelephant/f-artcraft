# Field plate: Earthstar

Status: draft, not posted. Theme: [After the Rain](../themes/after-the-rain.md).

## Concept

A single earthstar opens over damp leaf litter: a softly rounded spore sac
held above papery rays that curl away from the ground. Chalk, umber and a
cool slate disc. The central sac needs volume; the rays must not read as a
flat decorative star. Use a reference for the opening and ray structure.

## Composition and lettering

1000×1000; dark backdrop, gradient disc centred at 500,460 with r≈420.
Low three-quarter view, all rays and leaf litter inside the rim. Bound the
substrate to the disc's actual arc. Gradients, translucent damp highlights,
warm dark ink, no fantasy glow or edibility claims.
Virelai Sans title `EARTHSTAR` at y≈930, ~56–60pt; second line
`SPORE SAC · SPLIT RAYS` at y≈968, ~22–24pt, tracked, ≤760pt wide.
