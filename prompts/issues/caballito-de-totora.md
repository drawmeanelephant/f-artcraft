# Watercraft — field plate: caballito de totora (Peru)

## Brief

A Huanchaco-style caballito de totora: bundled reeds form a narrow
upturned prow and broader working stern, resting beside a simple
paddle. North-coast Peruvian fishing heritage, not a Lake Titicaca reed
boat or a generic pan-Andean raft. Warm straw, sea-green disc, a small
contained water reflection. Use the actual reed bundle and lashing
structure; do not invent a dragon-headed bow or sail.
