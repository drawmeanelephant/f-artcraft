# Bureau scene: The Moth at the Complaints Desk

Status: draft, not posted. Theme: [The Bureau After Hours](../themes/bureau-after-hours.md).

## Concept

A living moth on the visitor side of an empty complaints counter faces
the desk lamp as if it were the official hearing the case. One blank form
lies between them. The lamp's warm pool does the emotional work; beyond it,
an idle ticket dispenser and dark wall suggest an office that has gone home.
Deadpan and tender, not a cartoon face on every object.

This is an original scene, not an archive entry, numbered mascot or quoted
poem. No invented lore IDs. Use a reference for a plausible resting moth.

## Composition and lettering

Landscape 1500×1000, no plate disc. Keep all essential shapes and type at
least 60pt from canvas edges. Counter edge leads toward the moth and lamp;
make the moth large enough to read without a close-up.
Navy/olive darkness, amber light, gradients and translucent shading, warm
dark ink rather than pure black. Virelai Sans `COMPLAINTS` on a small desk
sign; bottom title `THE MOTH AT THE COMPLAINTS DESK`, fitted within the safe
margin at a readable size. No explanatory paragraph in the artwork.
