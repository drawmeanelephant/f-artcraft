# Animal — field plate: jaguar (Central America)

## Brief

Close a large-cat gap with a Belize/Central American forest reading of
*Panthera onca*. A heavy-jawed adult turns toward the viewer, shoulder in
three-quarter view, amber eyes under wet leaves. Deep green disc, warm
ochre coat. Rosettes wrap the body and contain irregular inner spots:
do not turn it into a leopard. Wildlife, not an invented Maya emblem.

## Format and delivery

1000×1000; dark backdrop, gradient disc centred 500,460, r≈420. Keep the
subject and habitat inside the rim. Gradients and translucent shading;
warm dark ink, not pure black. Virelai Sans `JAGUAR` near y930 (~56–60pt),
`PANTHERA ONCA` near y968 (~22–24pt, tracked, ≤760pt wide).

Commit `art/animals/jaguar/jaguar-V1R00.vectorcraft`, `.svg`, `@2x.png` and
driver `work/animals/jaguar-V1R00.py`. PDF stays local at
`work/out/jaguar-V1R00.pdf`.
