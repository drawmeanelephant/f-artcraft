# Holidays — wave 2 expansion proposal: the wider year

Status: draft, not posted. One leaf issue per row when approved;
`type:illustration` + `work:original`. See `.github/LABELS.md`.

Festival Seal grammar (`prompts/series/40-orient.md`): 1000×1000, gradient
disc centred 500,460 r≈420, name line ~54–58pt at y≈934, a date/epithet
second line ~22–24pt tracked at y≈970 (e.g. `JULY 14 · FÊTE NATIONALE`),
small vermilion chop top-right outside the disc. Warm ink, holiday-specific
palette, gradients and translucent shading. Objects and settings, not
worshippers; no national flags, maps or political symbols. Drivers in
`work/holidays/`; shared kits `cn_kit.py` and `festival_kit.py`.

Wave 1 shipped 34 seals: the Chinese and Indian rosters plus the wider
calendar. Wave 2 opens the year to the festivals the first pass skipped.
The tagging follows wave 1's loose buckets (religious / government / food)
with `folk` and `seasonal` added where nothing fits — tagging is a filing
aid, not doctrine. Slugs checked against `art/holidays/` and open/closed
issues on 2026-10-25; none collide.

Watch-items:

- `tet` and `spring-festival` share the lunar new year; the leaf must earn
  the split — peach blossom, bánh chưng and lì xì are the Vietnamese
  emblem set, not lanterns and dumplings redrawn.
- `halloween` is folk register, not horror: candlelit gourds, no gore, no
  movie-monster vocabulary.
- `loy-krathong` sits near `songkran` (both Thai); water is shared, the
  emblem is not — floating basket vs water festival.
- Date lines carry real dates or the festival's own epithet; movable
  feasts use the season line (`FIRST FULL MOON OF SPRING`), not a guess.
