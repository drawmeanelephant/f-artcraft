# Field plate: Hand plane

Status: draft, not posted. Theme: [Quiet Tools](../themes/quiet-tools.md).

## Concept

A generic bench hand plane in a low three-quarter view: polished wood
knob and rear handle, iron body, a credible blade assembly, and one curled
shaving. Walnut warmth against a smoky blue disc. Wood grain follows the
form; the shaving is a thin curved surface, not a floating ribbon icon.
Use a reference for the blade, lever cap and mouth.

## Composition and lettering

1000×1000; dark backdrop, gradient disc centred at 500,460 with r≈420.
Object, shaving and soft contact shadow stay inside the rim. Leave the
blade assembly air; essential edges ≥3pt. Gradients and translucent shading,
warm dark ink, no maker badge or white chrome bars.
Virelai Sans title `HAND PLANE` at y≈930, ~56–60pt; second line
`IRON · STEEL · WOOD` at y≈968, ~22–24pt, tracked, ≤760pt wide.
