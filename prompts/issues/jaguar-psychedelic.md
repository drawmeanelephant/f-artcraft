# Animal — field plate: jaguar, psychedelic poster rendition (V2R00)

## Brief

A 60s concert-poster reading of the jaguar: rosettes become radiating
concentric rings in vibrating pink/yellow/teal, the coat is a gradient field
instead of ochre, the background is a pulsing colour-wash. Same subject as
the existing Central American field plate (V1R00), but taken fully into the
psychedelic idiom. Wildlife anatomy preserved — it's still a jaguar, just
hallucinating.

## Composition and lettering

1000×1000 canvas. No mandatory plate disc — a radiating psychedelic field
fills the frame within a 60pt safe margin. Jaguar in three-quarter view,
shoulder toward viewer, amber eyes under radiating colour rings. Deep
purple/vibrating green backdrop. Rosettes as concentric neon rings that
wrap the body without turning it into a leopard.

Lettering: "JAGUAR" warped along an arc in bold psychedelic display
treatment. Second line: "PANTHERA ONCA" tracked small beneath, readable at
@2x.

## Style notes (deliberate break of house rule #1)

Rendition, not a house plate: flat and gradient fills allowed, translucent
colour overlays encouraged, warm-dark-ink rule replaced by a psychedelic
palette. Nothing clipped at canvas edges, nothing leaking past the 60pt
margin.
