# Field plate: Maidenhair fern

Status: draft, not posted. Theme: [The Night Garden](../themes/night-garden.md).

## Concept

One airy maidenhair frond, arching from lower left toward upper right,
fan-shaped leaflets catching green-gold transmitted light. Deep petrol
disc, warm dark stems, restrained dew. Draw a living structure, not a fern
symbol or an ornamental border; use a reference for leaflet attachment.

## Composition and lettering

1000×1000; dark backdrop, gradient disc centred at 500,460 with r≈420.
Leave breathing room inside the rim; all leaflets and stem ends stay in it.
Use ≥3pt for essential stems. Gradients and translucent shading throughout,
warm dark ink rather than pure black. Virelai Sans title `MAIDENHAIR FERN`
at y≈930, ~56–60pt; second line `FAN LEAFLETS · ARCHING FRONDS` at y≈968,
~22–24pt, tracked, ≤760pt wide. Check both lines on the actual proof.
