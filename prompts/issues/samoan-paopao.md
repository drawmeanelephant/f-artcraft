# Watercraft — field plate: paopao outrigger canoe (Samoa)

## Brief

A small Samoan paopao: dugout hull and a single outrigger float connected
by clearly arranged booms/lashings. Slight elevated profile so the float
does not disappear behind the hull. Warm timber over an indigo lagoon.
An everyday fishing craft, not a large ceremonial vessel, generic
“Polynesian canoe,” Hawaiian sailing canoe or Fijian double canoe.
Do not add a sail or decorative motifs absent from the selected reference.

## Format and delivery

1000×1000; dark backdrop, gradient disc centred 500,460, r≈420.
Fit hull, float, booms and paddle entirely inside the disc; reflection
ends on its arc. Gradients, translucent shading, warm dark ink;
essential booms/lashings ≥3pt. Virelai Sans `PAOPAO` near y930;
`DUGOUT · OUTRIGGER` near y968, ~22–24pt, tracked, ≤760pt wide.
