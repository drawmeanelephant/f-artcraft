# Commemoratives — wave 2 expansion proposal: instruments of the craft

Status: draft, not posted. One leaf issue per row when approved;
`type:illustration` + `work:original`. See `.github/LABELS.md`.

Companion-plate grammar (`prompts/series/30-companion-plates.md`): 1000×1000,
gradient disc centred 500,460 r≈420, name ~54–58pt at y≈930, epithet second
line ~22–24pt tracked at y≈968. The series commemorates software artifacts —
real and archive-native alike (precedent: `doom-engine` and `quicksilver`
are real; `boris`, `dogbed`, `swe2-dogbed` are ours). Each plate draws the
thing doing its thing, and the epithet must be earned by the drawing.

Wave 1 shipped nineteen plates. Wave 2 turns to the instruments underneath
them: the protocols, languages, formats and small tools the work actually
runs on — plus one in-universe instrument that has earned its monument.

Held in reserve: `t3-code` (THE SHARED THREAD) · `colossal-cave` (THE CAVE
BEHIND THE WORD) · `sed` (THE STREAM EDITOR) · `smalltalk` (THE MESSAGE
PASSER).

Watch-items:

- No living-person tributes and no competitor products; the real-software
  precedent is landmark artifacts, not endorsements.
- Project names appear as captions (the `doom-engine` precedent); brand
  logos, wordmarks and trademarks do not get drawn.
- One artifact per plate, shown working — these are commemorations, not
  headstones; the mourned dead belong to `art/necropolis/`.
- `flatlophone` is the wave's only in-universe leaf; if reviewers want the
  wave fully canonical, promote `t3-code` from reserve in its place.
