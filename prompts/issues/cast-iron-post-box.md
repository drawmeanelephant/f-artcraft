# Field plate: Cast-iron post box

Status: draft, not posted. Theme: [Small Infrastructure](../themes/small-infrastructure.md).

## Concept

A generic freestanding cast-iron post box after rain, softly rounded,
painted deep red, with a recessed slot and a small unlettered collection
panel. Three-quarter view over a little wet paving. No royal cypher, real
postal logo, council mark or address. The slot is an opening, not a stripe.

## Composition and lettering

1000×1000; dark backdrop, gradient disc centred at 500,460 with r≈420.
Keep the whole object inside the rim; paving and shadow end on the disc's
arc. Gradients and translucent rain highlights, warm dark ink; wear stays
on the painted surface, not across the silhouette.
Virelai Sans title `POST BOX` at y≈930, ~56–60pt; second line
`CAST IRON · ENAMEL` at y≈968, ~22–24pt, tracked, ≤760pt wide.
