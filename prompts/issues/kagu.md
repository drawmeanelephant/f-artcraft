# Animal — field plate: kagu (New Caledonia)

## Brief

*Rhynochetos jubatus* pauses on a shadowed forest floor, pale powder-grey
body lifted above red-orange legs, expressive crest partly raised.
New Caledonia, in the southwest Pacific. Folded wings for this first
plate: do not paint the normally concealed display pattern onto the
closed wing. A grounded flightless bird, not a heron standing in water.
