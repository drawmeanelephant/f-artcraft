# Animal — field plate: Andean condor (South America)

## Brief

An adult male *Vultur gryphus* stands on a high Andean ledge, wings folded,
head turned to show the comb and bare skin above the white neck ruff.
Dark feather masses need internal warm-grey planes, not a black cutout.
Cold dawn disc, one rim light. A wildlife portrait, without invented
ceremonial dress, national flags or a generic pan-Andean symbol collage.
