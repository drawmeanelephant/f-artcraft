# Animal — field plate: red-eyed tree frog (Central America)

## Brief

*Agalychnis callidryas* holds a rain-wet leaf stalk, one red eye facing us,
orange toe pads spread around the stem and a little blue/yellow flank
visible. Costa Rica/Panama rainforest focus. This is a distinct animal
from the existing glass frog and queued blue poison dart frog: no
transparent belly, no poison-dart body pattern. Luminous green, not neon.
