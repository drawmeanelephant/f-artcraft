# Animal — field plate: Cuban trogon (Caribbean)

## Brief

The tocororo, *Priotelus temnurus*, sits quietly on a forest branch,
turned enough to show blue-green upperparts, grey chest, red belly and
its distinctive tail. A Cuban-archipelago wildlife subject, not a copy of
the existing quetzal. Use reference-correct tail feather shapes rather
than extending them into quetzal streamers. No flag or political emblem.
