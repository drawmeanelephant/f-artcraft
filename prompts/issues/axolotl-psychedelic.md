# Animal — field plate: axolotl, psychedelic poster rendition (V3R00)

## Brief

A 60s concert-poster reading of the axolotl: vibrating complementary
colours, radiating colour waves from the feathery gills, wavy warped
lettering, dripping paint and halo rings. Same subject as the existing
house-style plates (V1R00 front portrait, V2R00 cold full-body tank), but
the psychedelic idiom owns the whole plate — not a gradient disc, not the
house palette. Maximum over-the-top energy.

## Composition and lettering

1000×1000 canvas. No mandatory plate disc — the composition is a radiating
psychedelic field that fills the frame, contained within a 60pt safe margin.
The axolotl sits centre in neon pinks/cyans against a deep purple or
vibrating green backdrop. Gills explode outward as radiating colour-wave
bands. Drips fall from the gill edges into the lower third.

Lettering: "AXOLOTL" warped along an arc or wavy baseline in a bold
psychedelic display treatment (install a psychedelic font — Permanent Marker,
Fredericka the Great, Nosifer or similar — or fake the warp with Virelai
Sans transformed letter-by-letter). Second line: "AMBLYSTOMA MEXICANUM"
tracked small beneath.

## Style notes (deliberate break of house rule #1)

This is a rendition, not a house plate: flat and gradient fills are both
allowed, translucent colour overlays are encouraged, and the "warm dark ink"
rule is replaced by a psychedelic palette. The one non-negotiable: nothing
clipped at canvas edges, nothing leaking past the 60pt margin.
