# Animal — field plate: marine iguana (Galápagos)

## Brief

*Amblyrhynchus cristatus* basks on a dark volcanic ledge after swimming.
Full three-quarter body with blunt snout, dorsal crest, stout gripping
feet and a laterally flattened tail. Charcoal-to-warm-grey scales, salt
marks used sparingly, turquoise reflected light. This is a Galápagos
marine iguana, not a yellow land iguana with an ocean backdrop.
