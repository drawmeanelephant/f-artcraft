# Animal — field plate: spectacled bear (South America)

## Brief

*Tremarctos ornatus* rests one forepaw on a mossy branch in Andean cloud
forest. Three-quarter bust with enough shoulder to read as a bear, and a
small bromeliad as habitat context. Cream facial markings vary between
individuals: follow one reference, not a perfectly symmetrical pair of
cartoon spectacles. Lift the dark coat with warm translucent highlights.
