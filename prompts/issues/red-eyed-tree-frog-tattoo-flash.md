# Animal — field plate: red-eyed tree frog, traditional American tattoo flash rendition (V2R00)

## Brief

A traditional American tattoo-flash reading of the red-eyed tree frog: thick
black outlines, limited palette (red, yellow, black, skin tone), iconic
pose, zero subtlety. The frog grips a rain-wet leaf stalk, one red eye
facing us, orange toe pads spread, a little banner underneath. Same subject
as the existing Central American field plate (V1R00), but rendered as flash
art — bold, iconic, unapologetically graphic.

## Composition and lettering

1000×1000 canvas. No mandatory plate disc — the flash composition sits
centre within a 60pt safe margin, on a flat or subtly gradient field.
Frog in bold silhouette with ≥6pt warm-black outlines, flat colour fills,
limited palette. A ribbon banner beneath the frog (traditional flash
element) can carry the name or stay blank.

Lettering: "RED-EYED TREE FROG" in bold block display, either inside the
banner or beneath it. Second line: "AGALYCHNIS CALLIDRYAS" tracked small.
All type in Virelai Sans or a bold display face; no gradients on type.

## Style notes (deliberate break of house rule #1)

Rendition, not a house plate: flat fills, thick outlines, limited palette.
The "gradients and translucent shading, never flat fills" rule is replaced
by the flash grammar — flat is the point. Nothing clipped at canvas edges,
nothing leaking past the 60pt margin. Essential toes ≥3pt.
