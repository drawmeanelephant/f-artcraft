# Watercraft — field plate: Chono dalca (southern Chile)

## Brief

A historical Chono dalca from the southern Chilean archipelagos:
long central plank and two bent side planks joined into a light canoe.
Show seam/fastening structure in a slight elevated near-profile view,
with one unbranded paddle. Weathered warm wood, cold green water.
The Chono source tradition matters: do not turn it into a generic
dugout, Viking boat or Pacific outrigger, or merge different historical
reconstructions without explanation.
