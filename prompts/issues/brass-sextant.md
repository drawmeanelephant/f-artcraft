# Field plate: Brass sextant

Status: draft, not posted. Theme: [A Cabinet of Measures](../themes/cabinet-of-measures.md).

## Concept

A brass sextant in a slight three-quarter view, suspended visually over a
soft shadow. A graduated arc, pivoted index arm and telescope form a
recognisable instrument. Teal midnight disc, aged brass and translucent
glass. Use a structural reference; no impossible gears or fake maker marks.

## Composition and lettering

1000×1000; dark backdrop, gradient disc centred at 500,460 with r≈420.
Keep the complete frame and telescope inside the rim. Essential arms and
graduations ≥3pt; simplify lesser marks rather than losing the silhouette.
Gradients and translucent shading, warm dark ink, no white chrome bars.
Virelai Sans title `BRASS SEXTANT` at y≈930, ~56–60pt; second line
`CELESTIAL NAVIGATION` at y≈968, ~22–24pt, tracked, ≤760pt wide.
