# Furniture — field plate: Masaya cotton hammock (Nicaragua)

## Brief

A handwoven cotton hammock from the Masaya craft tradition, empty and
hanging in a soft catenary. Show a documented suspension/spreader
arrangement, open weave and restrained fringe, not a stock striped beach
sling. Natural cotton with one muted colour band. This is a living
Nicaraguan craft, not a claim that all Central American hammocks look alike.
Do not copy a named artisan's decorative pattern.

## Format and delivery

1000×1000; dark backdrop, gradient disc centred 500,460, r≈420. Suspension
loops, cords and fringe all fit inside the rim; a minimal display support
may be used, with no trees cut off at the edge. Gradients, translucent
shading, warm dark ink, essential cords ≥3pt.
Virelai Sans `MASAYA HAMMOCK` near y930; `COTTON · WOOD` near y968,
~22–24pt, tracked, ≤760pt wide; omit WOOD if the chosen form has no spreaders.
