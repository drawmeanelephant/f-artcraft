# Insect — field plate: glasswing butterfly (Central America)

## Brief

A living glasswing butterfly, *Greta oto* in the cited identification
account, rests on a slim leaf with wings open enough to show transparent
panels, brown borders and the pale forewing band. The background must
remain visible through the wing windows; avoid turning it into two
glass shards with a body glued between them. Check the selected
reference's taxonomy, venation and wing outline.
