# Animal — field plate: ring-tailed lemur (Madagascar)

## Brief

*Lemur catta* sits on a low sun-warmed rock, amber eyes toward the viewer,
ringed tail curling beside and behind the body. Madagascar dry-forest
focus. Five-digit hands/feet, a dark muzzle and clear facial mask.
The tail is not prehensile: no branch-wrapping grip. A distinct addition
alongside existing aye-aye and fossa plates, not an invented island mascot.
