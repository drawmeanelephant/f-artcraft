# Zodiac — wave 2 expansion proposal: the Western seals

Status: draft, not posted. One leaf issue per row when approved;
`type:illustration` + `work:original`. See `.github/LABELS.md`.

`art/zodiac/` — the Celestial Seals — is complete: all twelve Chinese
animals shipped. Wave 2 proposes a **new sibling series**,
`art/western-zodiac/` with drivers in `work/western-zodiac/`, rather than
more plates in the finished set.

Proposed grammar: same seal chassis (1000×1000, gradient disc centred
500,460 r≈420, name ~54–58pt at y≈934, second line ~22–24pt tracked at
y≈970, vermilion chop top-right) with its own dressings, parallel to the
Chinese set:

- Disc keyed to the sign's triplicity element — Fire red, Earth ochre,
  Air silver, Water blue — echoing the Chinese fixed-element discs.
- A ring of the twelve zodiac glyphs (♈…♓) inside the rim, replacing the
  earthly-branches ring; the sign's own glyph stamped in the chop.
- Second line: glyph + tropical date range (e.g. `♈ · MAR 21 – APR 19`).
- **Emblem-first**: where the sign is a person, the emblem does the work —
  sheaf, urn, bow, paired pillars — so all twelve stay in one register.

| slug | element | the seal's job |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `aries` | Fire | The ram's head, horns curling into the disc's own arc |
| `taurus` | Earth | The bull's head, garlanded, muzzle low |
| `gemini` | Air | Paired uprights/twin pillars mirroring one face between them |
| `cancer` | Water | The crab, claws raised, shell catching the rim light |
| `leo` | Fire | The lion's head, mane drawn as a sun corona |
| `virgo` | Earth | A bound wheat sheaf, ribbon loose at the knot |
| `libra` | Air | Balanced scales, chains and pans catching light |
| `scorpio` | Water | The scorpion, tail curled over a low body |
| `sagittarius` | Fire | A drawn bow, arrow nocked — the archer's equipment, not the archer |
| `capricorn` | Earth | The sea-goat: goat forequarters, scaled fish-tail curling |
| `aquarius` | Air | A tilted urn mid-pour, water arcing in two streams |
| `pisces` | Water | Two fish circling head-to-tail, nearly touching |

Watch-items:

- New series name `western-zodiac` is the proposal — it keeps
  `art/zodiac/` immutable and mirrors the `zodiac` ↔ `western-zodiac`
  pairing; alternatives (`sidereal-zodiac`, a vedic `nakshatra` set of 27)
  noted for maintainers, not offered here.
- No literal human figures: Gemini, Virgo, Sagittarius and Aquarius all
  resolve to emblems, keeping the "objects not worshippers" register the
  Orient series already holds.
- Element palette must not collide with the Chinese set's Wood/Fire/
  Earth/Metal/Water mapping at a glance — Air's silver reads next to
  Metal only on proofs, so check side-by-side before committing.
- Glyph ring lettering needs the same budget note as the Chinese branch
  ring: hand-placed glyphs, counted before drawing.
