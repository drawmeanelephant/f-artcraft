# Insect — field plate: blue morpho (Central/South America)

## Brief

A living blue morpho (*Morpho helenor peleides* in the Florida Museum
account) rests over a small fallen fruit, wings opening into an electric
blue gradient. Show blue dorsal surfaces and a restrained glimpse of
brown eyespotted underside only where the wing angle permits. The
colour reads as iridescent scales, not a flat cyan sticker or a
glasswing's transparent window. Neotropical focus, not island endemism.

## Format and delivery

1000×1000; dark backdrop, gradient disc centred 500,460, r≈420.
Whole insect inside the rim, no specimen pin. Use layered gradients and
translucent shading, with venation subordinate to scale colour;
warm dark ink, essential legs/antennae ≥3pt.
Virelai Sans `BLUE MORPHO` near y930; `LEPIDOPTERA` near y968,
~22–24pt, tracked, ≤760pt wide.
