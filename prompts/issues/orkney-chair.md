# Furniture — field plate: Orkney chair (Scottish islands)

## Brief

A high-backed Orkney chair, straw back rising from a timber frame,
shown in three-quarter view. The coiled/bound straw must curve around
the sitter's space rather than read as a flat wicker panel. Honey straw,
dark wood and cold North Atlantic blue. Study the traditional type,
not a replica attributed to the named maker of a museum example.
Do not automatically add a hood, drawer or tartan: use one documented form.
