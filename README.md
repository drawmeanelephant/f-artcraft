# f-artcraft

Vector art, prompts, and the round-robin loop — drawn live in VectorCraft by
Fart Knocker for drawmeanelephant. For giggles, but with commit history.

## The loop

**Prompt → build → export PDF/PNG proof → written analysis → fold the analysis
into the next prompt → next round.** A prompt is done when its piece survives
its own critique. The prompt bank and protocol live in
[PROMPTS.md](PROMPTS.md); the baseline critique of the work so far is
[ANALYSIS-ROUND-0.md](ANALYSIS-ROUND-0.md).

House rules: gradients and translucent shading, never flat fills; check proof
edges before calling anything finished; lettering in Virelai Sans; ornaments
from the Virelai Mascots glyphs. Guest and correspondent work — other agents,
people who don't natively draw, paint and mixed-media artists — is received
with appreciation, not critique; the red pen is for in-house rounds only.

## What's here

- **art/** — the canonical home of every piece, versioned `<slug>-V<V>R<RR>`
  (native + `.svg` + `@2x.png`), always `art/<series>/<slug>/`. Series work —
  `art/animals/`, `art/insects/`, `art/mascots/`, `art/vehicles/`,
  `art/furniture/`, the Orient sets — sits beside `art/pieces/` (the named
  works: Fart Knocker self-portrait and crest, the Filed & Forgotten set,
  Ember dusk, Empathegy), `art/muse/` (Jolly, the regular Muse mascot:
  portrait, waving, and a meeting with a very small Fart Knocker) and
  `art/guest/` (work from other agents: Freebuff's DeepSeek whale in six
  styles, and a dusk poster).
- **work/** — the scripts that drove VectorCraft's MCP server. Drawing
  scripts live next to their subject: `work/<series>/` mirrors `art/`,
  `work/tools/` holds shared scratch utilities. `vc.py`, `mcp_client.py`
  and `check_rim.py` at the root are the shared harness.

## The site

[https://drawmeanelephant.github.io/f-artcraft/](https://drawmeanelephant.github.io/f-artcraft/)
— the gallery, generated by dogbed: `docs/gen_data.py` scans the artwork into
`docs/data/*.json`, `docs/src/*.knap` renders through `docs/build.sh` into
`docs/site/`, and the `pages` workflow rebuilds and deploys it to GitHub
Pages on every merge to main. Generated output is never committed — new
art reaches the site automatically; the `docs-build` check just proves
the render still runs.

## What's not here

The `.vectorcraft` natives, PDF proofs, and PNG renders are binary and live in
the local archive — the SVGs in this repo are the public, viewable form of
every piece. Nothing here is a promise. Bad manners, good engineering.
